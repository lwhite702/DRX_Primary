# AI Intelligence - Quick Start Guide

## Welcome to AI-Powered Pricing & Inventory Management

This guide helps you get started with the AI Intelligence features in DRX CRM.

## What Can AI Intelligence Do?

### 📊 Smart Pricing
- **AI Suggested Prices**: Get intelligent pricing recommendations based on your costs, sales velocity, and inventory levels
- **Price Ranges**: See recommended min/max prices to stay competitive
- **Why Explanations**: Understand why AI suggests each price change
- **One-Click Apply**: Accept recommendations and automatically update prices

### 📈 Inventory Forecasting
- **Stockout Predictions**: Know exactly when you'll run out of stock
- **Restock Quantities**: AI calculates optimal reorder amounts
- **Demand Trends**: See if sales are accelerating or slowing
- **Risk Alerts**: Get warned about overstock or critical low stock

### ⚠️ Smart Alerts
- **Overstock Warnings**: When you have too much inventory relative to sales
- **Stockout Alerts**: When stock is running low given current sales pace
- **Demand Spikes**: When sales suddenly accelerate
- **Margin Risks**: When pricing threatens your profit margins

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Access AI Intelligence
1. Log into DRX CRM
2. Click **"AI Intelligence"** in the main menu
3. You'll see the Intelligence Dashboard with key metrics

### Step 2: Understand Your Dashboard

**Pricing Recommendations**
- Shows number of suggested price changes waiting for review
- Higher number = more optimization opportunities

**Critical Alerts**
- Shows urgent issues requiring immediate attention
- Red alert = review needed

**Urgent Restocks**
- Products that need reordering soon
- Orange alert = plan next week's order

**Total Opportunities**
- Sum of all optimization opportunities
- Best place to start your day

### Step 3: Analyze Your Products

1. **Search for a Product**
   - Use the search box to find by SKU or product name
   - Or scroll through your inventory list

2. **Click "Analyze"**
   - Wait a few seconds for AI to process
   - You'll see two recommendation cards

3. **Review Pricing Recommendation**
   - **Current Price**: What you're charging now
   - **Recommended Price**: What AI suggests
   - **% Change**: Price increase or decrease
   - **Range**: Acceptable price boundaries
   - **Why**: Reason for the recommendation

4. **Review Inventory Forecast**
   - **Current Stock**: Units on hand
   - **Reorder Level**: Your safety stock threshold
   - **Projected Stockout**: When you'll run out (if current trend continues)
   - **Recommended Restock**: How many units to order
   - **Urgency Level**: How soon you need to act

## Common Scenarios & Actions

### Scenario 1: High Demand, Low Stock
**Signals:**
- "Demand acceleration detected" in forecast
- Stockout projected in 7-14 days
- Red/orange urgency badge

**Action:**
1. Note recommended restock quantity
2. Contact supplier immediately
3. Consider temporary price increase (if AI suggests it)

### Scenario 2: Overstock, Low Demand
**Signals:**
- "Overstock risk" in forecast
- Stagnant sales or declining trend
- Purple/yellow urgency badge

**Action:**
1. Review AI pricing recommendation (likely discounted)
2. Consider promotional pricing
3. Plan clearance if stock is too old

### Scenario 3: Stable, Healthy Inventory
**Signals:**
- "Ample stock supply" in forecast
- Stable demand trend
- Blue/green urgency badge

**Action:**
- No urgent action needed
- Monitor weekly for changes
- Review pricing optimization opportunity

### Scenario 4: New Product, No Sales History
**Signals:**
- "No sales history" in pricing analysis
- Stagnant velocity trend
- Confidence score < 50%

**Action:**
1. Start with AI suggested price
2. Monitor for first 2 weeks of sales
3. AI will improve recommendations as data accumulates

## Making Decisions

### When to Apply AI Pricing
✅ **Apply When:**
- Confidence score is > 70%
- Your cost has changed significantly
- Demand trend is clear
- You want to optimize margins

❌ **Don't Apply When:**
- Confidence is < 50% (insufficient data)
- It contradicts your business strategy
- You're in a promotional period
- The change exceeds your comfort level

### Pricing Decision Tips
1. **Cost Increases**: Usually safe to apply - maintains margin
2. **Demand Spikes**: Good opportunity to increase price
3. **Overstock**: Consider the discount depth and duration
4. **Competition**: AI doesn't see competitors - use judgment
5. **Promotions**: Temporarily override AI recommendations

### Inventory Decision Tips
1. **Lead Time Matters**: Longer supplier lead time = order earlier
2. **Seasonal Patterns**: AI learns these over time
3. **Volatile Demand**: Use recommended range, not exact number
4. **Cash Flow**: Order smaller batches if tight on cash
5. **Storage Limits**: Don't exceed warehouse capacity

## Understanding AI Confidence Scores

**90-100%** - Very Confident
- Trust this recommendation
- Make immediate decisions based on it
- Clear, consistent data pattern

**70-89%** - Confident
- Safe to follow most of the time
- Good data foundation
- Minor uncertainties

**50-69%** - Moderate
- Use as guidance, not absolute truth
- Include judgment calls
- Watch results closely

**Below 50%** - Low Confidence
- Insufficient data
- Keep baseline approach
- Wait for more sales history

## Reading the Audit Trail

Every AI decision is logged for transparency:

1. **Who Made the Decision**: User or system
2. **What Changed**: Old value → New value
3. **Why**: Complete reasoning
4. **When**: Timestamp of change
5. **Override Reason**: If you rejected AI suggestion

To view audit trail:
- Click "View Details" on any recommendation
- Scroll to "Decision History"
- See all past recommendations and changes

## Troubleshooting

### "No recommendations generated"
**Reason**: Product has no sales history
**Solution**: Either start sales or update unit cost/price first

### "Pricing seems too high/low"
**Reason**: AI might be optimizing for margin, not volume
**Solution**: Use "Review Details" and consider overriding with your strategy

### "Forecast date seems wrong"
**Reason**: Unusual recent sales pattern
**Solution**: Wait a few days of normal sales for better accuracy

### "Analysis is taking too long"
**Reason**: Analyzing entire inventory takes time
**Solution**: Try single product analysis or check back in 5 minutes

### "Alert won't go away"
**Reason**: You need to click "Acknowledge"
**Solution**: Review the alert and click Acknowledge button
