This guide explains how security safeguards work in DRX and clarifies the difference between everyday account security and emergency data procedures.

## What this page is for

Use this guide when a team member asks about emergency data removal, device loss procedures, or account safety controls.

This guide prevents costly mistakes by explaining the actual safeguards built into DRX.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Store Manager or Store Owner.
- **Key screens**:
  - **Help Center** (`/help`): Official support and emergency escalation.
  - **Manage Team** (`/settings/team`): Deactivate employee accounts.
  - **Active Devices**: Remotely sign out lost or compromised devices.
- **Emergency ground rule**:
  - Never perform hasty destructive actions on active store accounts during counter rush hours. Contact store ownership and official support first.

## Fast path

1. **Lost or stolen mobile device**:
   - Open **Settings** -> **Active Devices**.
   - Find the missing phone or tablet and tap **Sign Out Device**.
   - This immediately locks the device out of your store workspace and revokes all cached register access.
2. **Former employee departure**:
   - Open **Manage Team** (`/settings/team`).
   - Tap the employee's name and select **Deactivate Account**.
   - Their PIN and login are immediately invalidated across all registers.
3. **Suspected inventory count tampering**:
   - Open **Stock Audit Trail** (`/inventory/audit`).
   - Review recent adjustments to see exact timestamps, operator names, and reason notes.
4. **Official store closure or data erasure requests**:
   - Data erasure and workspace closure are handled through verified ownership support channels, not an accidental single-tap button on cashier registers.

## If blocked

- **Staff member asks for a "panic wipe" button**:
  - DRX does not provide an unauthenticated "one-tap self-destruct" on cashier screens, as this would create severe operational risk for store owners.
  - Device sign-out and account deactivation are the proper, controlled safety tools.
- **Lost internet connection during shift**:
  - Do not panic or attempt to reset the app. Mobile registers continue taking orders offline and upload automatically once your connection returns.

## What is beta here

- Automated multi-store incident escalation workflows are in preview testing.
- Remote device sign-out, instant staff deactivation, and detailed stock audit trails are fully live.
