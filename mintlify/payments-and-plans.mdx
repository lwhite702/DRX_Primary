This page explains how tender methods and customer credit behavior work in live DRX routes.

## What this page is for

Use this guide when your team needs clear payment handling across normal checkout and credit follow-up work.

This page focuses on **tender correctness**, **credit safety**, and **what to do when a payment path is blocked**.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**:
  - Cashier: normal tender handling
  - Supervisor/manager: exception handling and credit follow-up
- **Routes used**:
  - `/sales`
  - `/pos/payment`
  - `/credit`
  - `/sales/[id]`
- **Live tender options**:
  - cash
  - card
  - credit
  - zelle
  - mixed
- **Credit rule**:
  - Credit tender is tied to customer context and store rules.

## Fast path

1. **Select payment method** during checkout.
2. **Enter required references** for methods that need it.
   - Example: zelle confirmation text.
3. **Confirm totals before submit**.
   - Verify subtotal, tax, fees, and final amount.
4. **Submit once and verify result**.
   - Confirm sale success state and sale ID.
5. **For credit activity**, open `/credit`.
   - Record extension or payment with customer selected.
   - Confirm updated balance and recent activity entries.
6. **After shift close**, spot-check payment history in sale detail routes.

## If blocked

- **No payment method selected error**:
  - Select tender first, then submit.
- **Credit unavailable**:
  - Attach customer.
  - Confirm credit eligibility and role scope.
- **Zelle submit fails**:
  - Enter required reference note.
  - Retry after validating connection and payload.
- **Mixed tender confusion in lane**:
  - Confirm operator process and amount split policy before submit.
- **Possible duplicate payment risk after timeout**:
  - Check if a sale ID was already created.
  - Do not post a second transaction until status is confirmed.
- **Credit balance looks wrong**:
  - Verify recent extension/payment actions in `/credit` and corresponding sale record.

## Tender-specific tips

- **Cash**: confirm amount handling and change process by shift policy.
- **Card**: confirm card path completed before moving to next customer.
- **Credit**: confirm customer and eligibility before submit.
- **Zelle**: always record reference value for follow-up.
- **Mixed**: confirm split math before final submit.

## Closeout checks

- Spot-check at least three transactions by tender type.
- Confirm credit activity from shift is visible in `/credit`.
- Flag any unclear tender notes before shift handoff.

## Manager handoff checklist

At handoff, manager confirms:

- No unresolved duplicate-payment questions remain.
- Credit entries from shift have matching context notes.
- Zelle references are present where used.
- Tender exceptions were reviewed and accepted.

## Training notes for new cashiers

- Learn one primary tender path first.
- Practice credit path only after customer attach behavior is clear.
- Ask for supervisor help before forcing a workaround in live lane.
