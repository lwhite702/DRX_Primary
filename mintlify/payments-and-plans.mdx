This guide explains how to accept different payment methods at checkout, handle electronic transfers, and manage customer store credit accounts.

## What this page is for

Use this guide to ensure payment accuracy, prevent double-charges at checkout, manage customer credit balances, and close out shift cash drawers.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**:
  - Cashier: Ring up sales, calculate cash change, and accept card/transfer payments.
  - Supervisor or Manager: Extend credit limits, receive credit balance payments, and void transactions.
- **Key screens**:
  - **Payment Screen** (`/pos/payment`): Select customer tender during checkout.
  - **Credit Center** (`/credit`): View customer balances, credit limits, and payment logs.
  - **Sales History** (`/sales`): Review past receipts and payment breakdowns.
- **Supported payment methods**:
  - **Cash**: Instant change calculation with \$10, \$20, \$50, \$100 quick buttons.
  - **Card**: Credit or debit terminal processing.
  - **Store Credit**: Deducts directly from an attached customer's approved credit line.
  - **Zelle / Bank Transfer**: Digital payments requiring a confirmation reference note.
  - **Split Tender**: Combine cash and card (or credit) on a single purchase.

## Fast path

1. **Select payment method at checkout**:
   - Tap **Pay** on the register cart.
   - Choose the payment method the customer wants to use.
2. **Enter transfer reference for Zelle**:
   - When accepting Zelle or digital transfers, enter the transaction ID or sender name for evening reconciliation.
3. **Verify totals before tapping Complete**:
   - Confirm subtotal, tax, delivery fee, and change due.
4. **Complete the sale**:
   - Tap **Complete Order** once. Hand the customer their receipt and change.
5. **Managing store credit in the Credit Center** (`/credit`):
   - **Extend Credit**: Select a customer to grant or increase their credit limit.
   - **Log Credit Payment**: When a customer comes in to pay down their tab, select their profile, enter the cash/card amount, and tap **Record Payment**.
   - The customer's balance updates instantly.
6. **Shift closeout spot-check**:
   - Compare your end-of-shift drawer cash against the cash total reported in **Operations Insights**.

## If blocked

- **"Select a Payment Method" prompt appears**:
  - Tap a tender button (Cash, Card, Credit, etc.) before tapping Complete Order.
- **Store Credit option is disabled**:
  - Attach a customer profile to the cart and verify that they have an active credit line with available balance.
- **Zelle transfer will not submit**:
  - Type the customer's transfer confirmation note into the reference field.
- **Screen froze while processing card payment**:
  - Do not immediately swipe the card again. Check the **Sales** list to see if the receipt was generated to avoid double-charging the customer.
- **Customer's credit balance looks incorrect**:
  - Open the **Credit Center** (`/credit`) and review the customer's payment history and recent credit transactions.

## Best practices for cashiers

- **Cash**: Always count change out to the customer or use the quick cash calculator.
- **Cards**: Wait for the reader's confirmation sound before handing the customer their items.
- **Transfers (Zelle)**: Check your store's banking app to verify funds have arrived before completing the order.
- **Store Credit**: Make sure the person standing at the counter matches the authorized customer on file.
