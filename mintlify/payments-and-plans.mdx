# Payments and plans

Deelrx CRM handles transaction payments in **Sales** and account balances in **Credit Management**.

## What this does

- Takes payment via **Cash**, **Card**, **Credit**, **Zelle**, and **Mixed**
- Records payment references when required
- Tracks credit extension and payment transactions

## Why this matters

Payment accuracy protects cashflow and customer trust.

## Step-by-step

1. In **Sales**, set the **Payment Method**.
2. Enter references for methods that need one (for example **Zelle**).
3. Complete checkout.
4. For account balances, open **Credit Management**.
5. Use extension/payment dialogs to update customer credit history.

## Common mistakes

- Posting sale as **Credit** when it should be immediate payment
- Forgetting payment reference when required by your process
- Not reconciling credit activity daily

## Edge cases

- Reassigning customer on credit sales may require manager/owner access.
- Partial payment handling can affect remaining balance visibility.

## Current limitations

- Billing/subscription plan management is not exposed in DRX app pages.
