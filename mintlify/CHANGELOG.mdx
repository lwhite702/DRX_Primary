Updates that make daily sales, stock management, and customer store accounts easier, clearer, and faster to run.

DRX rolls out updates continuously. Depending on whether your team uses web, phone, or tablet, features may appear on different screens as updates finish.

## 2026-09-17 — v0.9.4

### Improved

- **Smooth business setup on mobile:** Starting a new business account from an iPhone or iPad moves directly into your workspace without getting stuck during setup.
- **Clearer order totals:** Point-of-sale discounts and adjustments calculate instantly so the final charge matches your receipt before you accept payment.

## 2026-09-17 — v0.9.3

### New

- **Quick cart adjustments:** Remove an individual item from the register cart with a single tap, or clear the entire cart with a quick confirmation.
- **Add-to-cart confirmation:** Adding a product shows an immediate, gentle confirmation banner so cashiers know an item was scanned or tapped without second-guessing.
- **Cart badges on product cards:** Product tiles now display how many units are already sitting in the register cart.
- **Dedicated text message consent:** Customers and staff can opt in to important store alerts and support text messages with clear privacy choices.

### Improved

- **Faster, more reliable sign-in:** The sign-in screen loads more quickly, with stronger field visibility and high-contrast styling so you can get right to work.
- **In-app help and feedback:** Send feedback or report an issue directly from within DRX whenever you need assistance.
- **Smoother setup wizard:** Setting up a new shop guides you step by step and alerts you clearly if any required detail was skipped.

## 2026-08-30 — v0.6.12

### New

- **DRX Help Center:** Guides for sales checkout, deliveries, inventory intake, customer store credit, and staff access now live in one central place at `docs.deelrxcrm.com`.

### Improved

- **Dependable checkout and sales reports:** Sales totals and daily reports always match, preventing missing or duplicate receipts.
- **Phone and tablet navigation:** Menus and action buttons are sized comfortably for touch on handheld screens.
- **Direct support access:** Get in touch with customer support directly from the app or help center.

## 2026-08-24 — v0.6.8

### Improved

- **Saved checkout state:** Your register cart stays exactly as you left it when moving between item selection and the payment screen.
- **Accurate checkout totals:** Taxes, delivery fees, and discounts always display clearly, even when an optional discount is not used.
- **Higher contrast on payment screens:** Payment buttons and receipt cards are easier to read under bright store lighting.

### Fixed

- Cashiers without checkout permissions see a clear explanation instead of an endless loading spinner.

## 2026-08-23 — v0.6.6

### Improved

- **Clearer sign-in fields:** Email and password fields feature crisp borders and bright typing cursors across web and mobile screens.

## 2026-08-23 — v0.6.1

### Improved

- **Dependable offline selling:** DRX handles brief internet interruptions more gracefully so you can keep serving customers at the counter without losing sales data.

## 2026-08-23 — v0.6.0

### New

- **Sell by weight or spend:** Sell products by quantity, weight, or custom spend amount, with convenient one-tap preset buttons.
- **Order deliveries:** Track customer deliveries with delivery addresses, fulfillment status, and assigned team members.
- **Reliable checkout recovery:** If a network drop occurs during checkout, DRX preserves the sale safely without charging twice.

### Improved

- Inventory stock adjustments, customer balance lookups, and sales summaries handle slower connections more cleanly.

## 2026-07-22 — v0.5.7

### Fixed

- Finishing initial store setup takes you straight into your store dashboard instead of reloading the setup page.

## 2026-05-31 — v0.5.5

### New

- **Privacy protections for customer accounts:** Keep customer records minimal and automatically clean up inactive accounts based on your store's privacy preferences.
- **Accurate stock history:** Stock intake and manual count adjustments save with a clear record of who made the change and why.

## 2026-02-14 — v0.5.0

### New

- **Quick Sale counter checkout:** A fast, focused point-of-sale screen to add products, accept cash or card, and print or text receipts.
- **Customer store balances:** Keep track of who paid, who has credit, and who has an outstanding store balance due.

If you have questions about any update or want help setting up a feature in your store, reach out anytime at [support@deelrxcrm.com](mailto:support@deelrxcrm.com).
