This runbook covers the fast counter register flow: **Item Selection**, **Payment Tender**, and **Order Completion**.

## What this page is for

Use this guide when cashiers are ringing up lines of customers quickly and need the fastest checkout path with minimal screen taps. It is designed specifically for busy walk-in counter retail.

Use this page for training new cashiers, morning shift huddles, and lane troubleshooting.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Cashier or above.
- **Register state**:
  - The correct business is open on the device.
  - Barcode scanner or camera is connected and ready.
  - Sellable items are active in stock.
- **Balance due rule**:
  - If a customer is paying with balance due, attach their profile before tapping Pay.
- **Lane caution**:
  - Never press the charge button repeatedly if a payment terminal is processing. Confirm payment status first to avoid charging a customer twice.

## Fast path

1. **Open New sale** (`/pos/sale`):
   - Access the fast register from the main menu or bottom navigation bar.
2. **Add products to the active cart**:
   - Scan barcodes with your handheld scanner or device camera.
   - Or type a product name/nickname in search, or tap favorite product tiles.
   - For custom or unlisted items, enter a manual price line item.
3. **Adjust quantities**:
   - Tap an item in the cart to increment quantity or adjust weight.
   - Swipe or tap delete on unwanted items before moving to payment.
4. **Attach customer (optional)**:
   - Tap **Add Customer** if they are redeeming loyalty rewards or using balance due.
   - Skip for standard cash or card walk-ins.
5. **Proceed to Payment** (`/pos/payment`):
   - Tap the bright green **Pay** button at the bottom of the cart.
6. **Choose payment method**:
   - Select **Cash**, **Card**, **Balance due**, **Zelle**, or **Split**.
   - For cash, select quick bills ($10, $20, $50, $100) to see exact change due.
7. **Complete order & print receipt**:
   - Tap **Complete Order**.
   - Select printed receipt, text receipt, or no receipt.
   - The screen shows a green success badge and automatically resets for your next customer.

## If blocked

- **Barcode scan does not find product**:
  - Type the item name or SKU into search.
  - If still missing, verify with a manager that the item is listed in **Products** with available stock.
- **Item shows "Out of Stock"**:
  - Do not ring up an alternative SKU without manager approval.
  - Open **Receive Stock** (`/inventory/intake`) or **Adjust Stock** (`/inventory/adjust`) to correct shelf counts.
- **Cannot tap the Pay button**:
  - Ensure there is at least one item in the cart.
  - If an item requires manual weight or price input, make sure a value was entered.
- **Balance due option unavailable**:
  - Attach the customer to the sale and check their balance limit.
- **Screen reset quickly after tapping complete**:
  - This is expected behavior! The register resets automatically after showing the confirmation badge so you can immediately scan the next customer's items.

## Lane coaching tips for new cashiers

- Scan items first, ask for customer phone number only if they request loyalty points.
- Use quick cash buttons to calculate change instantly without mental math errors.
- If a card reader takes a few seconds, wait for the terminal chime before taking any action.
- When your shift ends, tap your profile avatar to lock the register for the next cashier.
