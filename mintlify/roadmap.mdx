A look at what we are actively building and planning for DRX. Our priorities focus on keeping sales fast, counts accurate, and customer accounts simple to manage.

## Now (In Active Development)

- **Clear active and inactive balance totals**
  Dashboard and Payments figures use the same active-account definition, while balances on inactive accounts remain visible in a separate total.
- **Create or attach customers during checkout**
  Add a customer from the payment step without leaving the sale, or link a customer to a completed anonymous sale from transaction review.
- **Accurate first-run setup progress**
  Home reflects saved products and other completed setup steps for the active business, and offers a retry when progress cannot be refreshed.

- **Easier product setup across devices**
  Choose automatic or your own SKU, set selling amounts without conflicting quick buttons, and calculate cost when you buy by the ounce but sell by the gram. Web rollout and native app verification are tracked separately.
- **Bundle prices for product quantities**
  Set exact totals for larger amounts while adding a product, such as 2g for $60 or 3.5g for $90. Checkout and device verification are part of this work.
- **Exact dollar-amount checkout for fractional products**
  Keep the requested merchandise amount exact while the operator reviews or edits the suggested fractional quantity. Flag price differences for the sale record without blocking checkout, and use the confirmed quantity for stock and cost of goods. Cross-platform checkout and audit verification are in progress.
- **Dedicated Hardware & Barcode Scanner Pairing**  
  Direct support for Bluetooth thermal receipt printers and wireless handheld barcode scanners at the checkout counter, speeding up high-volume transactions.

- **Fast Inventory Intake & Batch Scanning**  
  Quick-scan stock intake that lets managers scan multiple items in sequence, verify package counts, and print batch labels without extra counter steps.

- **Multi-Register Counter Flow**  
  Smoother handoffs when multiple staff members ring up sales simultaneously on separate iPads or registers in the same shop.

- **Direct Text Receipts & Order Status**  
  Fast text message (SMS) delivery for customer purchase receipts and pickup/delivery fulfillment notices.

## Next (Planned)

- **Customer Digital Menu & Pre-Ordering**  
  A clean, mobile-friendly online menu where customers can build an order for in-store pickup or local delivery. Operators confirm available stock before payment is accepted, avoiding out-of-stock headaches.

- **Multi-Location Inventory Balancing**  
  Transfer stock between multiple storefronts or warehouses with clear transfer manifests and instant count adjustments.

- **Customer Loyalty Points & Store Credit Rewards**  
  Reward frequent customers with automated loyalty points that redeem directly for discounts or store credit at checkout.

- **Expanded End-of-Day Balancing & Cash Drawer Auditing**  
  Simplified cash drawer reconciliation, shift summaries, and daily discrepancy reports for shop owners and managers.

## Later (In Research)

- **Automated Sales Tax & Accounting Exports**  
  One-click formatted sales and tax summary exports tailored for store accountants and bookkeepers.

- **Integrated Delivery Courier Dispatch**  
  Direct dispatch to on-demand courier services for stores that offer local customer delivery without a dedicated in-house driver.

- **Low-Stock Alerts & Restock Recommendations**  
  Proactive notifications when top-selling products run low, with suggested reorder quantities based on your store's sales pace.
