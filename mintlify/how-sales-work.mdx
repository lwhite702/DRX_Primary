# How sales work

Deelrx CRM runs checkout from the **Sales** screen with live cart totals and payment handling.

## What this does

- Product search and add-to-cart
- Optional customer assignment
- Taxed totals and misc fees
- Checkout with **Cash**, **Card**, **Credit**, **Zelle**, or **Mixed**

## Why this matters

Sales is where inventory, customer balances, and revenue all update together.

## Step-by-step

1. Open **Sales**.
2. Search products and click **Add**.
3. Adjust quantity or remove line items.
4. Select customer if needed.
5. Choose **Payment Method**.
6. Add payment reference when using **Zelle**.
7. Confirm totals and complete checkout.

## Common mistakes

- Trying to check out with an empty cart
- Selling more than available stock
- Forgetting to attach a customer for credit scenarios

## Edge cases

- Weight-based products can require measured entry before adding.
- Random inventory checks can trigger during sales operations.
- Manager/owner access is required for some reassignment and override actions.

## Current limitations

- Sale reassignment rules are stricter for credit sales.
- Advanced reporting on sales trends is not built out in **Reports** yet.
