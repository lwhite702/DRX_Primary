# How sales work

This page covers the standard **Sales** screen flow for full checkout handling.

## What this page is for

Use this guide when your team runs checkout from `/sales` and needs the full cart workflow with customer attach, delivery context, fees, and tender selection.

If your cashier lane uses the fast register flow, use [Quick Sale runbook](/quick-sale-runbook) instead.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Cashier or above for standard sales; supervisor/manager for restricted actions.
- **Required route**: `/sales`
- **Needed data state**:
  - At least one sellable inventory item
  - Optional customer profile (required for credit use)
  - Tax behavior already set in settings
- **Know your role limits**:
  - Price overrides, void-style actions, and some reassignments can require higher roles.

## Fast path

1. **Open Sales** (`/sales`).
2. **Add items** by search and cart actions.
3. **Set quantity** or remove line items as needed.
4. **Attach customer** when needed.
   - Optional for normal cash/card sales.
   - Required when using credit tender.
5. **Set payment method**.
   - Supported methods in this flow: cash, card, credit, zelle, mixed.
   - Add payment reference when needed (for example zelle confirmation).
6. **Review totals**.
   - Check subtotal, tax, fees, and total before submit.
7. **Complete checkout**.
   - Confirm success message and sale record ID.
8. **Verify side effects**.
   - Inventory moved as expected.
   - Customer balance updated if credit used.

## If blocked

- **Cannot checkout**:
  - Confirm cart has at least one item.
  - Confirm stock is available.
  - Confirm payment method is selected.
- **Credit method unavailable**:
  - Attach a customer.
  - Confirm credit is allowed for current context.
- **Zelle flow rejected**:
  - Enter payment reference field when prompted.
- **Price override not available**:
  - Role likely lacks override permission.
  - Escalate to manager/supervisor route.
- **Totals look off**:
  - Check tax toggle in settings.
  - Check for fee rows and delivery charge entries.
- **Checkout error appears after click**:
  - Retry once and check whether sale already completed before trying again.

## Shift checklist

Use this quick list before and after each shift:

- Confirm tax behavior is expected in first test cart.
- Confirm one cash sale and one card sale can complete.
- Confirm customer attach works from lookup.
- Confirm credit method is only used when customer context is present.
- Confirm last completed sale ID is visible for dispute follow-up.
- At close, spot-check one sale detail route for tender correctness.

## Operator notes

- Keep one clear team rule for when to use standard Sales vs Quick Sale.
- If your store allows manual fees or delivery charges, define who can apply them and when.
- Keep route and click-path notes for recurring issues so new staff can recover faster.
