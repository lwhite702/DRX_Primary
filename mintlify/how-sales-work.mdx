This page covers the standard **Sales** screen for full order management and counter checkout.

## What this page is for

Use this guide when ringing customer sales from the **Sales** tab (`/sales`) when you need full order options—such as attaching detailed customer notes, managing delivery address fields, adding custom delivery fees, or splitting tenders.

For high-speed counter lanes ringing quick walk-ins, see the [New sale runbook](/quick-sale-runbook) instead.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Cashier or above for ringing sales; Supervisor or Manager for manual discounts and voids.
- **Screen**: **Sales** (`/sales`).
- **Required information**:
  - Sellable products in stock with active stock.
  - An attached customer profile if accepting balance due as payment.
  - Active tax settings (configured in Store Settings).
- **Staff permissions**:
  - Price overrides and manager voids may require a supervisor PIN depending on your store rules.

## Fast path

1. **Open Sales** (`/sales`):
   - Access the main sales register from the navigation bar.
2. **Add products to the cart**:
   - Search by product name, SKU, or nickname, or browse categories.
   - Adjust quantities or remove line items as needed.
3. **Attach a customer profile (optional)**:
   - Walk-in cash or card sales do not require a customer profile.
   - Attach a customer if they want receipt texts, loyalty points, or are paying with balance due.
4. **Choose payment tender**:
   - Select **Cash**, **Card**, **Balance due**, **Zelle**, or **Split Payment**.
   - For electronic transfers like Zelle, enter the transfer confirmation code for shift reconciliation.
5. **Review receipt totals**:
   - Double-check the subtotal, applicable taxes, delivery charges, and final balance.
6. **Complete the sale**:
   - Tap **Complete Sale** once.
   - A green confirmation badge with the receipt number will appear on screen.
7. **Verify stock and balance**:
   - Products counts deduct automatically.
   - If balance due was used, the customer's remaining balance updates immediately.

## If blocked

- **Cannot tap the checkout button**:
  - Check that there is at least one item in the shopping cart.
  - Verify that no item is listed as out-of-stock.
  - Ensure a payment method has been selected.
- **Balance due option is greyed out**:
  - Attach an active customer profile to the cart first, and ensure they have an approved balance limit.
- **Zelle transfer submission fails**:
  - Enter the reference or confirmation note from the customer's transfer app.
- **Cannot discount an item or change price**:
  - Your account may need a manager override. Call a supervisor to enter their PIN.
- **Sales totals look incorrect**:
  - Check whether sales tax is enabled in Store Settings and verify if custom delivery fees were added to the order.
- **Register lagged after tapping Complete**:
  - Check your recent sales list to see if the transaction already posted before attempting to charge the customer again.

## Shift opening and closing checklist

- Ring a quick 1-cent test transaction at opening to verify card terminal and cash drawer response.
- Confirm tax rate is calculating properly on the subtotal.
- Verify customer search finds active profiles quickly.
- At closing, spot-check your payment breakdown in **Trends** against your drawer cash and terminal batch slips.
