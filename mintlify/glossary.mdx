---
title: "DRX glossary"
description: "Current product names and common terms used across DRX guides."
icon: "book-open"
---

- **Deelrx CRM**: the formal product name.
- **DRX**: the primary name used inside the product and Help Center.
- **Inventory**: products, received goods, current counts, adjustments, and related records.
- **Point of Sale**: the checkout workspace used to build and complete a sale.
- **Orders & Receipts**: the saved-sale history used to confirm items, customer state, payment record, and totals.
- **Store Credit**: an open customer balance recorded in DRX. DRX records the balance and payment method; it does not itself move money.
- **Workspace** or **business**: one business record with its own team access and operating data.
- **Alias Mode**: the privacy preset with a default 30-day inactive-customer retention window.
- **Standard Mode**: the privacy preset with a default 180-day inactive-customer retention window.
- **Full Customer Mode**: the privacy preset with a default 365-day inactive-customer retention window.
- **Retention cleanup**: the process that permanently deletes qualifying inactive customer records when it runs.
- **DRX Intelligence**: beta suggestions that must be checked against saved business records.
