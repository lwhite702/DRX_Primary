# Glossary

Deelrx CRM terms you will see inside DRX.

## Core navigation terms

- **Operator Dashboard**: The main operating snapshot page.
- **Sales**: Checkout flow for creating and completing transactions.
- **Inventory**: Product list and stock management area.
- **Customers**: Customer profiles, contact info, and account context.
- **Credit Management**: Credit limits, extensions, and payment tracking.
- **Event Desk Board**: Live metrics for sales, inventory alerts, and intake status.
- **Reports**: Reporting page. Current state: available page with limited output.
- **Settings**: Business profile, tax behavior, POS security, and team links.
- **Help & Support**: In-app support guidance and issue reporting.

## Inventory terms

- **Inventory Intake**: Workflow for recording incoming stock, cost, and weight.
- **Inventory Adjustment**: Manual stock correction with required reason.
- **Inventory Audit Log**: Time-ordered history of stock changes.
- **Product Aliases**: Nicknames mapped to standard units/quantities.
- **Reorder level**: Threshold used to surface low-stock risk.

## Sales and payment terms

- **Payment Method**: Checkout type. DRX supports Cash, Card, Credit, Zelle, and Mixed.
- **Sale Details**: Record page for one completed sale.
- **Price override**: Authorized manual price change during checkout.
- **Delivery option**: Optional delivery profile and charge attached to a sale.

## Customer and credit terms

- **Credit extension**: Increasing a customer's credit exposure.
- **Credit payment**: Payment applied to reduce outstanding balance.
- **Outstanding balance**: Current unpaid credit amount.

## Privacy and access terms

- **Privacy preset**: Data visibility profile selected during setup.
- **Team Management**: Role assignment page for staff access control.
- **Role permissions**: What each role can view or change.

## Safety terms

- **Offline sync queue**: Local transaction queue used when internet is down.
- **RTBF deletion**: Owner/super admin initiated user deletion workflow.
