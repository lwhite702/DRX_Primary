Key terms used in live DRX routes.

## Core navigation terms

- **Dashboard**: Main operational snapshot route.
- **Quick Sale**: Fast register flow for lane speed (`/pos/sale`).
- **Sales**: Full checkout workflow with broader cart controls.
- **Deliveries**: Delivery fulfillment and courier dispatch queue (`/deliveries`).
- **Inventory**: Product and stock management routes.
- **Customers**: Profile and customer history routes.
- **Credit**: Credit balance and transaction management.
- **Operations Insights**: Live metrics route for sales, stock alerts, and intake status.
- **Reports**: Reporting route with currently limited output.
- **Settings**: Business, team, privacy, and control settings.
- **Help Center**: Canonical documentation hub link.

## Inventory terms

- **Inventory intake**: Record incoming stock, cost, and weight.
- **Inventory adjustment**: Manual count correction with reason.
- **Inventory audit**: History of stock movement actions.
- **Reorder level**: Threshold used to flag low stock risk.

## Sales and payment terms

- **Tender method**: How a transaction is paid (cash, card, credit, zelle, mixed).
- **Fractional checkout**: Precision weight-based item sale with reciprocal dollar presets.
- **Bulk price tier**: Automatic price breaks applied at quantity thresholds (eighth, quarter, half, ounce).
- **Sale details**: Route showing a single completed sale.
- **Price override**: Controlled manual price change during checkout.

## Customer and credit terms

- **Alias-first profile**: Customer profile using alias without requiring full identity fields.
- **Loyalty rewards**: Reward point deductions applied at register.
- **Credit extension**: Increase in allowed credit exposure.
- **Credit payment**: Payment that reduces outstanding credit balance.
- **Outstanding balance**: Current unpaid amount.

## Privacy and safety terms

- **Privacy profile**: Field visibility and recommendation mode.
- **Mask mode**: On-screen hiding behavior for sensitive customer data.
- **Temporary profile**: Customer profile set to expire by retention policy.
- **Vault mode**: Key ownership pattern where keys are not stored in database tables.
- **Session revoke**: Manual termination of active account session.
