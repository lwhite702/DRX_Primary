A reference guide to common terms and concepts used across DRX screens and documentation.

## Navigation and screen terms

- **Home**: Today’s sales, totals, low-stock items, and other work that needs attention.
- **New sale**: Add products, attach a customer when needed, take payment, and save the sale.
- **Sales**: Past sales and receipts.
- **Deliveries**: Orders that need a handoff or delivery update.
- **Products**: Your product list, prices, and stock counts.
- **Customers**: Customer profile directory supporting alias nicknames, order history, and loyalty points.
- **Payments**: Customer payments, limits, and balances due.
- **Trends**: Patterns and suggestions that may need a closer look.
- **Settings**: Store profile configuration, tax rates, staff role permissions, and privacy choices.
- **Help Center**: Official documentation hub and direct customer support portal.

## Product and stock terms

- **Add stock**: Record products or quantities that came in.
- **Fix count**: Change a stock count and add the reason.
- **Stock history**: See each stock change, the old count, the new count, and who made it.
- **Low-stock limit**: The count that tells DRX a product is running low.

## Sales and payment terms

- **Payment Tender**: The payment method used by a customer (Cash, Card, Balance due, Zelle, or Split Tender).
- **Fractional Checkout**: Weight-based precision selling for goods sold by grams, ounces, or pounds.
- **Dollar Presets**: Quick \$10, \$20, \$35, \$50 buttons that automatically calculate the exact weight of product to weigh on the scale.
- **Bulk Price Tier**: Automatic volume price breaks applied at quantity thresholds (such as an eighth, quarter, half ounce, or full ounce).
- **Manager Override / PIN**: Security requirement where a supervisor enters their PIN to approve a discount, void, or manual price change.

## Customers and payments

- **Alias-First Profile**: A customer profile saved with a nickname or first name, without forcing cash walk-ins to provide phone numbers or addresses.
- **Loyalty Rewards**: Store discount points earned on previous purchases that can be redeemed at checkout.
- **Balance Limit**: The most a customer may owe before DRX warns or blocks another balance-due sale.
- **Balance due**: The money a customer still owes the business.

## Privacy and security terms

- **Hide details**: Cover phone numbers and customer details on shared screens.
- **Temporary Customer Profile**: A walk-in profile configured to automatically expire after 1, 7, or 30 days of inactivity.
- **Remote Device Sign-Out**: Instantly locking and signing out a lost phone, tablet, or former employee's browser.
