A reference guide to common terms and concepts used across DRX screens and documentation.

## Navigation and screen terms

- **Dashboard**: Real-time snapshot of today's store revenue, transactions, and alert summaries.
- **Quick Sale**: High-speed counter register designed for fast barcode scanning, cash calculation, and rapid checkout.
- **Sales & Orders**: Full-service order management screen for complex carts, customer notes, delivery details, and split tenders.
- **Deliveries**: Courier fulfillment screen for packing orders, dispatching drivers (like Uber Direct), and tracking drop-offs.
- **Inventory**: Your store's product catalog, pricing, reorder thresholds, and stock counts.
- **Customers**: Customer profile directory supporting alias nicknames, order history, and loyalty points.
- **Credit Center**: Management screen for tracking customer store credit limits, outstanding balances, and credit payments.
- **Operations Insights**: Live performance metrics tracking gross sales, transaction counts, average order values, and inventory intake status.
- **Settings**: Store profile configuration, tax rates, staff role permissions, and privacy profiles.
- **Help Center**: Official documentation hub and direct customer support portal.

## Inventory terms

- **Receive Stock (Intake)**: Logging incoming supplier shipments, invoice costs, and received weights or quantities.
- **Adjust Stock**: Updating a physical shelf count up or down with a clear explanation note (e.g. recount or breakage).
- **Stock Audit Trail**: The permanent timestamped history showing every stock change, previous count, new count, and staff member responsible.
- **Reorder Level**: The minimum threshold that triggers a low-stock alert on your dashboard so you can reorder before running out.

## Sales and payment terms

- **Payment Tender**: The payment method used by a customer (Cash, Card, Store Credit, Zelle, or Split Tender).
- **Fractional Checkout**: Weight-based precision selling for goods sold by grams, ounces, or pounds.
- **Dollar Presets**: Quick \$10, \$20, \$35, \$50 buttons that automatically calculate the exact weight of product to weigh on the scale.
- **Bulk Price Tier**: Automatic volume price breaks applied at quantity thresholds (such as an eighth, quarter, half ounce, or full ounce).
- **Manager Override / PIN**: Security requirement where a supervisor enters their PIN to approve a discount, void, or manual price change.

## Customer and credit terms

- **Alias-First Profile**: A customer profile saved with a nickname or first name, without forcing cash walk-ins to provide phone numbers or addresses.
- **Loyalty Rewards**: Store discount points earned on previous purchases that can be redeemed at checkout.
- **Credit Limit**: The maximum allowable store credit balance a trusted customer can charge to their account.
- **Outstanding Balance**: The current unpaid balance a customer owes on their store credit account.

## Privacy and security terms

- **Counter Screen Masking**: Partially hiding phone numbers and customer details on counter registers with asterisks to protect privacy from onlookers.
- **Temporary Customer Profile**: A walk-in profile configured to automatically expire after 1, 7, or 30 days of inactivity.
- **Remote Device Sign-Out**: Instantly locking and signing out a lost phone, tablet, or former employee's browser.
