This page is the daily product and stock operating guide for DRX.

## What this page is for

Use this guide to keep your catalog clean, stock counts accurate, and lane sell-through smooth.

It covers how product records, intake records, adjustments, and audit history work together.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**:
  - Cashier: view and sell
  - Manager/supervisor: intake and stock corrections
- **Routes used in this flow**:
  - `/inventory`
  - `/inventory/intake`
  - `/inventory/adjust`
  - `/inventory/audit`
- **Required data**:
  - SKU and product name
  - Unit and price
  - Initial stock or intake plan
- **Important habit**:
  - Use intake and adjust routes instead of side edits when possible.

## Fast path

1. **Create or review product records** in `/inventory`.
   - Confirm SKU, name, and base sell values.
   - Confirm product can appear in lane search.
2. **Receive stock** in `/inventory/intake`.
   - Select product.
   - Enter total cost, total received weight, optional supplier, optional delivery cost.
   - Confirm cost-per-unit calculation before submit.
3. **Handle intake result**.
   - If intake posts right away, verify quantity update in inventory list.
   - If intake requires approval, track pending status and wait for manager action.
4. **Correct stock when needed** in `/inventory/adjust`.
   - Use clear reason text.
   - Use decrease, increase, or set-to action carefully.
5. **Review trail** in `/inventory/audit`.
   - Confirm each stock movement has clear context.
   - Use this route after any dispute.
6. **Pre-shift low-stock check**.
   - Review low-stock indicators before counter opens.
   - Refill or adjust before lane pressure starts.

## If blocked

- **Intake submit fails**:
  - Check product selection is valid.
  - Check cost and weight are positive values.
  - Retry once, then capture route + payload for support.
- **Intake says approval required**:
  - This is expected in some stores.
  - Do not re-submit duplicate intake entries.
  - Move pending item through approval workflow.
- **Cart cannot sell item that looks in stock**:
  - Re-open product and verify current quantity and unit mapping.
  - Check business context did not switch.
- **Audit trail has unclear entries**:
  - Team is likely using vague reason text.
  - Update shift policy: reason must explain what changed and why.
- **Alias/nickname search misses product**:
  - Check `/settings/inventory/aliases` for duplicates or disabled entries.

## What is beta here

- Purchase-order style rollups and deep export layers are beta-limited.
- Core inventory create/intake/adjust/audit routes are live and should be used for day-to-day operations.

## Shift checklist

- Check low-stock flags before opening lane.
- Confirm one recent intake posted as expected.
- Confirm adjustment reason quality from prior shift.
- Confirm at least one top seller has accurate quantity.
- Confirm alias mappings still match what staff say at the counter.

## Team quality standards

- Every adjustment reason must explain cause, not just outcome.
- Intake entries should include enough cost detail for manager review.
- If a SKU repeatedly drifts, open a manager-level review and stop patching the same item silently.
