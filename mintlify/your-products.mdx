This guide explains how to manage your product catalog, receive stock shipments, and correct stock counts in DRX.

## What this page is for

Use this guide to keep your store shelves organized, stock counts accurate, and product searches responsive on the sales floor.

It covers creating products, receiving incoming vendor shipments, logging manual stock adjustments, and reviewing audit trails.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**:
  - Cashier: Browse products and sell items.
  - Supervisor or Manager: Receive shipments and log stock adjustments.
- **Key screens**:
  - **Products** (`/inventory`): View and edit your product catalog.
  - **Receive Stock** (`/inventory/intake`): Log incoming vendor deliveries and costs.
  - **Adjust Stock** (`/inventory/adjust`): Log shrinkage, recount corrections, or damage.
  - **Stock Audit Trail** (`/inventory/audit`): View the complete history of every stock change.
- **Required product details**:
  - Product name, SKU/barcode, selling price, and unit of measurement (each, g, oz, lb).

## Fast path

1. **Add or update products** (`/inventory`):
   - Add the product name, barcode SKU, retail price, and reorder warning level.
   - Attach a product nickname (e.g. "Green Tea 500ml") for faster search at the register.
2. **Receive stock shipments** (`/inventory/intake`):
   - Select the product being received.
   - Enter the total invoice cost, received weight or quantity, and optional vendor name.
   - Confirm the calculated cost-per-unit before saving.
3. **Verify updated stock**:
   - Stock quantities update automatically and become available at all registers immediately.
   - If your store requires manager approvals for new shipments, the delivery appears in the pending intake queue until approved.
4. **Correct counts when needed** (`/inventory/adjust`):
   - If shelf counts differ from the system, select the item in **Adjust Stock**.
   - Choose **Increase**, **Decrease**, or **Set Count to**.
   - Enter a brief explanation (e.g., _"Physical recount on shelf 2"_ or _"Broken bottle during restock"_).
5. **Review the stock audit trail** (`/inventory/audit`):
   - See who changed stock, when it was changed, the previous count, new count, and the reason.
6. **Check low-stock warnings**:
   - Items below their reorder point show an alert on Home so you can restock before running out.

## If blocked

- **Cannot save a stock intake shipment**:
  - Ensure the selected product is active and total cost and received quantity are greater than zero.
- **Shipment shows "Pending Approval"**:
  - This is expected if your store requires a manager to sign off on incoming supplier invoices. Ask a store manager to review and approve the delivery.
- **Item shows in stock on shelf but says 0 at checkout**:
  - Open the item in **Products** to verify its digital count.
  - If a physical count was not logged, submit a quick stock adjustment to bring digital stock up to date.
- **Cashiers cannot find a product by search**:
  - Check the **Product Aliases** list in Settings to make sure customer slang or nicknames are linked to the correct SKU.

## Daily store habits

- Run a quick low-stock check each morning before opening the front doors.
- Log vendor deliveries immediately upon arrival rather than waiting until the end of the day.
- Always include a clear reason note when adjusting stock counts to keep your audit records clean.
