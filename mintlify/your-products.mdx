# Your products

Deelrx CRM manages products through **Inventory**, **Inventory Intake**, **Inventory Adjustment**, and **Inventory Audit Log**.

## What this does

- Add and edit products
- Record intake cost and received weight
- Adjust stock with reasons
- Keep a log of stock movement
- Manage nickname mappings in **Product Aliases**

## Why this matters

Clean product data keeps sales fast and protects your margins.

## Step-by-step

1. Open **Inventory** and create products.
2. Use **Inventory Intake** for incoming stock and supplier/cost entry.
3. Use **Inventory Adjustment** for corrections with a reason.
4. Review **Inventory Audit Log** for change history.
5. Use **Manage Aliases** when your team uses unit nicknames.

## Common mistakes

- Manually editing around intake instead of recording intake properly
- Skipping reasons on adjustments
- Forgetting to verify unit mappings before selling by weight

## Edge cases

- Intake can route to approval flow when required.
- Out-of-stock items are blocked from cart add.
- Deleted products may appear as historical records in logs.

## Current limitations

- **Reports** currently does not expose full inventory reporting pages.
