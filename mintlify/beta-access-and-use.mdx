# Beta access and use

Deelrx CRM is currently operated as a beta product in active environments. In DRX, you can see this in the in-app **BETA** badge.

## What this means

- Features can change between releases.
- UI and workflow details can be updated quickly.
- Some pages are intentionally limited while core flows stabilize.

## How beta access works

1. Access is tied to your authenticated account and business context.
2. New behavior may be gated by internal feature controls.
3. Support and bug reporting is available in-app through **Report a Bug** and issue ticket flows.

## Expected operator behavior in beta

- Validate totals before finalizing checkout.
- Review **Inventory Audit Log** regularly.
- Report defects with exact steps, page, and error text.
- Keep role permissions tight while testing new workflows.

## Common mistakes in beta

- Treating preview behavior as final policy
- Rolling out new workflow to all staff without a shift-level test
- Reporting issues without reproduction steps

## Current limitations

- Some areas are intentionally minimal. Example: **Reports** currently shows "No reports yet."
