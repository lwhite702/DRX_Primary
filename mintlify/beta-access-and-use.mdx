Welcome to the cutting edge of modern retail operations! DRX is actively evolving alongside high-volume dispensary and retail operators. Being an early-access user means you get the newest features first—along with a healthy dose of common sense when testing new workflows.

Here is how to get the most out of early access without disrupting your store's daily grind.

## The beta operator playbook

- **Look for the beta badge**: Features labeled **beta** (like Purchase Orders or AI velocity recommendations) are functional and actively improving. Always sanity-check suggestions against your actual invoices.
- **Practice on dummy data first**: Training a new budtender or testing a barcode scanner? Use a practice item or demo workspace rather than cluttering your store's live financial ledgers.
- **Don't panic if an interface tweaks**: We refine screens based on real operator feedback from the counter. If a button moves slightly, check the section guide or reach out to support.

## Keeping your shop bulletproof

Even the slickest cloud POS in the world can't prevent a backhoe from digging up your neighborhood fiber line. As a savvy retail operator:
- **Run daily reconciliations**: Close out your registers every evening using **Daily Summary** or **End of Shift** reports and compare drawer cash against your receipts.
- **Keep paper backups handy**: Maintain basic offline receipt slips and tally sheets for unexpected power outages.
- **Check device requirements**: Verify your hardware (Bluetooth scanners, receipt printers, and iPads) on the test counter before setting up customer lines.
