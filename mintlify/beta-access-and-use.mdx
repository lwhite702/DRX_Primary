DRX regularly introduces new preview features alongside our core, battle-tested checkout registers and inventory tools. This guide explains how to safely use preview tools in your store.

## What preview features mean for your store

- Preview features give your team early access to upcoming capabilities before general release.
- Screen layouts and button designs in preview areas may evolve as we gather feedback from store operators.
- Your store's core register and inventory tracking remain stable and unaffected.

## Best practices for floor operations

1. **Keep core register operations on stable screens**:
   - Use standard **Quick Sale** and **Sales** for primary high-volume counter checkout.
2. **Test new tools with a single manager first**:
   - When trying a new preview workflow, have a store lead or manager test it before training all cashiers.
3. **Share feedback**:
   - Tell us what works and what could be improved by emailing `support@deelrxcrm.com`.

## What is in preview testing right now

- Advanced multi-store corporate rollouts and cross-location inventory pooling.
- Custom report builder export templates.
- Multi-stop automated delivery route clustering.

## What is fully live and battle-tested

- **Quick Sale Register** (`/pos/sale`): Rapid barcode scanning, cash calculator, card terminal processing.
- **Sales & Orders** (`/sales`): Full order creation, customer attachments, delivery quotes.
- **Deliveries** (`/deliveries`): Courier dispatch, status tracking, arrival alerts.
- **Inventory & Intake** (`/inventory`): Product catalog, vendor intake invoices, stock recount adjustments, shrinkage logs.
- **Customers & Credit** (`/customers`, `/credit`): Alias-first profiles, loyalty point redemption, customer credit lines.
- **Team Permissions & Security** (`/settings`): 4-tier staff roles, PIN overrides, screen masking.
