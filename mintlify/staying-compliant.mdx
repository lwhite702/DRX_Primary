# Stay audit-ready

This page is the operating playbook for clean stock records and clear adjustment history.

## What this page is for

Use this guide to keep inventory movement traceable, reduce variance surprises, and keep your team ready for internal or external record review.

The focus is practical: intake, adjustment, reason quality, and repeat-variance response.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**:
  - Cashier: view and report issues
  - Supervisor/manager: adjustments and shrinkage handling
- **Routes used**:
  - `/inventory/intake`
  - `/inventory/adjust`
  - `/inventory/audit`
- **Team rule**:
  - Every stock correction needs a clear reason that another person can understand without extra context.

## Fast path

1. **Use intake for received stock** (`/inventory/intake`).
2. **Use adjust for corrections** (`/inventory/adjust`).
   - Increase, decrease, or set-to with reason.
3. **Record shrinkage in the right flow**.
   - Do not hide shrinkage in vague notes.
4. **Review audit trail daily** (`/inventory/audit`).
   - Confirm who changed what and why.
5. **Check repeat variance patterns**.
   - If the same SKU shows recurring mismatch, escalate to manager review.

## If blocked

- **Adjustment rejected**:
  - Confirm role has inventory-manage scope.
  - Confirm payload values are valid.
- **Reason text is too vague**:
  - Replace generic text with root cause style: what changed, why it changed, who confirmed.
- **Random check finds mismatch**:
  - Pause further stock edits on that item.
  - Recount, correct, and document before normal selling resumes.
- **Audit trail missing expected entry**:
  - Recheck business context.
  - Confirm action completed, not aborted.

## Shift-close audit habit

- Review five recent audit entries for clarity.
- Confirm each manual correction has a real cause in reason text.
- Confirm shrinkage entries were not delayed to end of week.

## Escalation trigger

Escalate to manager review when any one of these repeats across shifts:

- Same SKU has recurring variance.
- Same operator submits vague correction reasons.
- Intake and actual shelf counts keep diverging.

## Reason text examples

Use these patterns:

- "Count correction after shelf recount, +12g, verified by shift lead"
- "Breakage during packaging, -3 units, logged same shift"
- "Intake entry typo corrected to posted invoice value"

Avoid patterns like:

- "fix"
- "update"
- "changed"

## New-staff coaching

- Teach adjustment reasons on day one.
- Teach where to review audit history before making second corrections.
- Teach when to stop and escalate instead of repeating manual stock edits.

## Daily close note

Write one short line at close describing the largest stock correction driver. This helps managers spot repeat causes across shifts.
