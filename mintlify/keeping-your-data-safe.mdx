This guide is your store's security runbook for keeping employee accounts secure, revoking old devices, and preventing customer data leaks at the sales counter.

## What this page is for

Use this guide to review active devices signed into your store, remove logins from employees who no longer work there, and ensure customer privacy settings are active during busy store hours.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Store Manager or Store Owner.
- **Key screens**:
  - **General Settings** (`/settings`): Privacy profiles, screen masking, and temporary customer retention.
  - **Manage Team** (`/settings/team`): Active staff accounts and role assignments.
  - **Active Devices**: Review currently active mobile and browser sessions.
- **Core security rule**:
  - Never share a single master manager password among employees. Give each cashier their own named login.

## Fast path

1. **Review team permissions monthly** (`/settings/team`):
   - Remove elevated supervisor or manager permissions from staff who no longer need them.
2. **Review customer privacy settings** (`/settings`):
   - Ensure counter screen masking is turned on so nearby shoppers cannot read customer phone numbers off the register screen.
   - Confirm temporary walk-in profiles are scheduled to expire after 1, 7, or 30 days.
3. **Inspect active logged-in devices**:
   - Check the list of active browsers and mobile devices.
   - If an unfamiliar tablet or phone appears, or a device was lost, tap **Sign Out Device** immediately.
4. **Respond to failed login alerts**:
   - If an employee account triggers multiple failed login attempts, assist them with a secure password reset and verify their identity.
5. **Establish a weekly manager security routine**:
   - Spend 5 minutes every Monday reviewing active staff members, signed-in devices, and privacy settings.

## If blocked

- **Cannot sign out an active device**:
  - Ensure you are signed in with a Manager or Owner account with administrative privileges.
- **Counter screen masking makes it difficult for cashiers to verify phone numbers**:
  - Train cashiers to ask the customer to read the last 4 digits of their phone number instead of turning off screen masking storewide.
- **Employee forgot their passcode or PIN**:
  - A manager can open the employee's profile in **Manage Team** and tap **Reset PIN**.

## Weekly security checklist for store managers

1. **Check active devices**: Sign out any old tablets, personal phones, or unused browsers.
2. **Audit staff list**: Deactivate any seasonal staff or former employees.
3. **Verify screen masking**: Ensure customer names and phone numbers are protected from counter onlookers.
4. **Check temporary profile retention**: Confirm walk-in customer records clean up as intended.
