# Keeping your data safe

Deelrx CRM protects operational data with role-based access, tenant scoping, and controlled update flows.

## What this does

- Restricts sensitive actions by role and permission
- Segments records by business context
- Limits settings and team changes to authorized users

## Why this matters

Most data incidents in store software are permission mistakes, not hacks.

## Step-by-step

1. Keep role assignments current in **Team Management**.
2. Limit who can access **Settings**, **Inventory Adjustment**, and credit actions.
3. Require clear reasons for stock changes.
4. Review logs and issue reports weekly.

## Common mistakes

- Shared operator accounts across staff
- Leaving elevated role access active after staffing changes
- Treating audit/history pages as optional

## Edge cases

- Users can see page shells but still be blocked on action by permission checks.
- Some advanced actions rely on both role and feature-specific guard logic.

## Current limitations

- Security analytics and policy dashboards are not exposed as dedicated DRX pages.
