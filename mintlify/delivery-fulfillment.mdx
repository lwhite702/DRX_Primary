This guide covers creating delivery orders at the register, scheduling courier pickups (like Uber Direct or your in-house drivers), and tracking packages until delivered.

## What this page is for

Use this runbook to manage the delivery queue in **Deliveries** (`/deliveries`), pack items accurately, hand off bags to couriers, and monitor customer arrival times.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Dispatcher, Cashier, Supervisor, or Manager.
- **Key screens**:
  - **New sale** (`/pos/sale`) or **Sales** (`/sales`): Create the delivery order.
  - **Deliveries** (`/deliveries`): Manage packaging, driver dispatch, and tracking.
- **Required information**:
  - Recipient drop-off address and contact phone number.
  - Delivery quote or estimated courier fee.
  - Delivery fee policy: whether the customer pays the delivery fee or your store covers it.

## Fast path

1. **Create delivery order at checkout**:
   - In **New sale** or **Sales**, turn on the **Delivery Order** switch.
   - Enter the drop-off street address, apartment/unit number, city, and zip code.
   - Enter the courier fee quote.
   - Choose who pays: select **Charge Customer** (adds fee to customer receipt) or **Absorb Fee** (your store covers the delivery expense).
   - Complete payment; the order is automatically sent to the **Deliveries** queue.
2. **Pack and prepare order (`Ready for Dispatch`)**:
   - Open **Deliveries** (`/deliveries`) and review the order packing slip.
   - Bag items securely and attach the printed receipt or order ticket.
   - Request a courier pickup (via Uber Direct or your in-house delivery fleet).
3. **Driver on the way (`Driver Dispatched`)**:
   - The driver's name, vehicle details, and estimated pickup time appear on the order card.
   - Your register or handheld device receives an alert when the driver is 2 minutes away.
4. **Hand off package (`Handed Off`)**:
   - When the driver arrives at the counter, confirm the customer name and order number.
   - Tap **Confirm Handoff** in the app.
5. **Customer receives package (`Delivered`)**:
   - The courier confirms drop-off at the customer's doorstep. The order completes automatically in your store records.

## If blocked

- **Driver is delayed or canceled**:
  - Tap the delivery order card and tap **Reassign Driver** or update pickup notes.
- **Customer calls to change drop-off address**:
  - If the package is still at the store, tap **Edit Address** on the order card.
  - If the driver is already on the road, message the courier directly.
- **Customer questions the delivery fee**:
  - Open the receipt summary in **Sales** to see whether the fee was added to their subtotal or covered by the store.
