# Delivery fulfillment

This guide covers end-to-end delivery orders, courier dispatch, fee allocation, and ETA monitoring in DRX.

## What this page is for

Use this runbook when fulfilling customer deliveries from `/deliveries`, scheduling courier handoffs (such as Uber Direct or in-house drivers), and tracking orders through final receipt.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Dispatcher, cashier, supervisor, or manager.
- **Required routes**:
  - `/pos/sale` or `/sales` (order creation with delivery tender toggle)
  - `/deliveries` (fulfillment queue and order lifecycle)
- **Required information**:
  - Delivery street address and optional recipient contact
  - Courier quote or estimated delivery fee
  - Fee policy: whether the fee is charged to the customer or absorbed by the merchant

## Delivery checkout flow

1. **Build the order** in Quick POS (`/pos/sale`) or standard Sales (`/sales`).
2. **Toggle "Delivery Order"**:
   - Enter destination street address, city, region, and postal code.
   - Enter courier delivery quote.
   - Select fee distribution:
     - **Charge customer**: adds courier fee and tax directly to order total.
     - **Absorb fee**: records courier cost as merchant expense while keeping customer total unchanged.
3. **Complete checkout**:
   - The order enters the live fulfillment queue in status `ready_for_dispatch`.

## Dispatch and tracking runbook

From `/deliveries`:

1. **Review incoming queue (`ready_for_dispatch`)**:
   - Verify packaged order items against receipt summary.
   - Order driver or dispatch courier via Uber Direct or internal logistics.
2. **Advance to `uber_ordered`**:
   - Enter courier driver identifier / tracking link and estimated pickup wait time.
   - Local and push notification reminders are scheduled (T-2m driver arrival alert).
3. **Advance to `handed_off`**:
   - Package is handed to driver at counter.
   - Driver signs or confirms custody.
4. **Advance to `delivered`**:
   - Courier confirms package handover at customer destination.
   - Order closes and final delivery record is sealed in audit logs.

## If blocked

- **Driver delayed or canceled**:
  - Update courier wait time or reassign driver identifier in delivery panel.
- **Customer address change**:
  - If before handoff, edit address in order detail view.
  - If after dispatch, contact courier support and record incident in audit notes.
- **Payment dispute on delivery fee**:
  - Check whether fee was marked as charged or absorbed in the original receipt breakdown.
