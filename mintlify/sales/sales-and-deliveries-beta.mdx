Running deliveries alongside your walk-in retail counter is a superpower, but it can turn into pure chaos if you lose track of who ordered what, where the driver is headed, and who is paying the courier fee.

DRX integrates delivery orders directly into your register flow so every drop-off has a clean address, an assigned runner, and an honest payment record.

## Adding delivery to a sale across platforms

### On Desktop Web (Browser)
1. Build your customer's cart in **New sale** as usual, then click **Continue to Payment**.
2. In the **Delivery** card, toggle on **Include delivery**.
3. Type the **Delivery address** and the quoted **Uber/vendor fee**.
4. Choose **Who pays the vendor fee?**:
   - **Charge customer**: Adds the courier fee (and any applicable delivery tax) onto the customer's cart total.
   - **Business absorbs**: Your store covers the delivery fee as an operational expense; the customer pays only for their merchandise.
5. Review the updated total, choose the customer's payment method, and click **Complete Sale**.

### On iPhone (Native iOS App)
1. On the **POS** screen, build the cart and tap **Checkout** to open the native checkout sheet.
2. Toggle on the **Delivery** switch.
3. Enter the drop-off destination address and courier fee.
4. Toggle **Charge Customer** on or off. The checkout sheet recalculates tax and total in real time.
5. Tap **Complete Sale** to save the order and spawn the active delivery task.

### On Android & Mobile Web
1. Open the cart in **Register** and tap **Checkout**.
2. Check the **Delivery Order** box to reveal the address and courier fee fields.
3. Confirm whether the customer is covering the runner fee, select payment tender, and complete the order.

## Managing your delivery board (`Deliveries`)

Once an order with delivery is saved, it automatically lands in your store's **Deliveries** board:

1. **Assigning the Runner**:
   - Click or tap **Deliveries** to see all active orders.
   - A driver or manager can tap **Assign to me** to take ownership of the drop-off.
   - For privacy, the customer's street address is masked on the main board. The driver taps **Reveal address (audited)** to view the destination when packing up.
2. **Dispatch Milestones**:
   - Tap **Mark ordered** once the courier or driver is dispatched.
   - Tap **Mark handed off** when the bag leaves the counter.
   - Tap **Mark delivered** when the customer confirms receipt.
3. **Cancellations & Adjustments**:
   - If a customer calls and cancels while the driver is still at the shop, an authorized manager can cancel the delivery task. Canceling the drop-off does not delete the sale; handle any needed refund or credit through normal register procedures.
