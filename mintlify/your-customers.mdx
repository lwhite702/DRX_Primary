This page covers customer records, lookup patterns, and safe credit-related customer handling.

## What this page is for

Use this guide when creating or updating customer profiles in `/customers`, attaching customers to sales, and keeping customer data minimal.

This page reflects live behavior: DRX supports alias-first profiles and optional contact fields.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**:
  - Cashier: customer lookup and attach
  - Supervisor/manager: customer edits and credit workflow support
- **Routes used**:
  - `/customers`
  - `/customers/[id]`
  - `/sales` and `/pos/sale` for attach behavior
  - `/credit` for balance context
- **Key data rule**:
  - You can create a valid profile with alias-only data.
  - Phone, email, first name, and last name are optional unless your own store policy says otherwise.
- **Privacy rule**:
  - Collect only what your team needs to run the sale and follow up on balances.

## Fast path

1. **Create profile** in `/customers`.
   - Enter alias if that is enough for your workflow.
   - Add contact details only when needed for follow-up.
2. **Save and verify**.
   - Confirm the profile appears in customer list.
   - Confirm you can open detail route.
3. **Attach in sales flow**.
   - Standard flow: attach from `/sales`.
   - Quick flow: attach from `/pos/sale` customer panel.
4. **Use detail page for context** (`/customers/[id]`).
   - Review history and credit context before extensions.
5. **Handle temporary customer records**.
   - If your privacy profile enables ephemeral records, confirm retention behavior in settings.
   - Use temporary records for fast walk-in operations where long-term identity is not needed.

## If blocked

- **Save fails on customer create**:
  - Check payload formatting.
  - Remove empty noise fields and retry with alias-only first.
- **Duplicate-like records appear**:
  - Standardize alias format for shift teams.
  - Add internal note convention for store-level disambiguation.
- **Cannot attach customer in lane**:
  - Confirm current business context.
  - Confirm customer is active and visible in lookup results.
- **Credit flow blocked after attach**:
  - Attach alone is not enough; credit eligibility must pass route checks.
- **Field visibility seems inconsistent**:
  - Privacy settings can hide fields by profile and mask mode.

## Shift checklist

- Confirm customer lookup works for at least one known profile.
- Confirm alias-only create still works for walk-in flow.
- Confirm credit attach flow can find active customer records.
- Confirm detail route opens without missing-field errors.

## Team policy notes

- Decide when alias-only is enough and when contact fields are required.
- Keep one naming pattern for aliases to reduce duplicates.
- Do not collect extra data by habit. Collect only what supports actual store work.
