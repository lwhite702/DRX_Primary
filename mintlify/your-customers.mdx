# Your customers

Deelrx CRM tracks customer profiles, contact details, sales history, and credit activity.

## What this does

- Create and edit customer profiles in **Customers**
- Open customer detail records for sales and credit context
- Connect customers to sales and credit limits

## Why this matters

Customer records keep credit risk, payment follow-up, and repeat business clean.

## Step-by-step

1. Open **Customers**.
2. Click add customer and enter profile details.
3. Enter **First name** and at least one contact method: phone or email.
4. Save and open the customer detail page when needed.
5. Use **Credit Management** to track limits and balances.

## Common mistakes

- Saving without phone or email
- Creating duplicate records with slight name variations
- Not assigning customer on sales that should hit credit history

## Edge cases

- Customer assignment can be updated from **Sale Details** with role controls.
- Some customer records can be active with partial profile data.

## Current limitations

- Customer-side messaging/automation flows are not exposed in-app.
