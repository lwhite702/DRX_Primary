This guide explains how to manage customer records, look up shoppers at checkout, and protect customer privacy during everyday counter transactions.

## What this page is for

Use this guide to create and update customer profiles in **Customers** (`/customers`), attach returning shoppers to orders, and keep customer records clean without collecting unnecessary personal data.

DRX supports an **alias-first design**, meaning you can create functional profiles using just a nickname or first name without demanding sensitive private information.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**:
  - Cashier: Search for existing customers and attach them to sales.
  - Supervisor or Manager: Edit profiles, extend credit limits, and review purchase history.
- **Key screens**:
  - **Customers** (`/customers`): Search and view all customer profiles.
  - **Customer Details** (`/customers/[id]`): View order history, loyalty points, and credit balance.
  - **Quick Sale** (`/pos/sale`): Attach a customer directly to an active counter order.
  - **Credit** (`/credit`): Manage outstanding balances and customer credit extensions.
- **Customer privacy policy**:
  - You can create a complete customer profile with an alias alone. Phone numbers, email addresses, and home addresses are entirely optional.

## Fast path

1. **Create a customer profile** (`/customers`):
   - Tap **Add Customer**.
   - Enter an alias or nickname (e.g. "Alex T.").
   - Add phone number or email only if the customer wants digital receipts or delivery updates.
2. **Attach customer at checkout**:
   - In **Quick Sale** or **Sales**, tap **Add Customer**.
   - Type their name, phone number, or alias in search.
   - Tap their name to attach them to the current cart.
3. **View customer details & purchase history**:
   - Open a customer profile to view previous receipts, favorite items, loyalty point balance, and payment history.
4. **Grant or manage store credit**:
   - If a trusted regular customer uses store credit, open the **Credit** tab on their profile to set an approved credit limit.
5. **Handle temporary walk-in profiles**:
   - If your store enabled temporary profiles in Privacy Settings, walk-in records automatically clean up after 1, 7, or 30 days of inactivity, keeping your database fast and clutter-free.

## If blocked

- **Cannot save a new customer profile**:
  - Check the fields you entered and ensure there are no formatting errors in optional phone or email fields. You can save with just a name or alias.
- **Multiple profiles appear for the same customer**:
  - Open the duplicate profile and merge or archive the inactive record to keep customer balances accurate.
- **Customer cannot be found in search**:
  - Search by phone number or alternative nickname.
  - Check that you are in the correct store workspace.
- **Store Credit payment is blocked after attaching customer**:
  - Check their profile in **Credit** (`/credit`) to confirm they have an approved credit limit and no overdue payment locks.
- **Phone numbers appear as `(***) ***-1234` on screen**:
  - Screen masking mode is turned on in Store Settings to protect customer privacy from shoppers standing at the counter.

## Store privacy principles

- Collect only the contact information your store actually needs to fulfill orders or provide receipts.
- Let walk-in cash shoppers remain anonymous or use a simple first name.
- Respect customer privacy by keeping screen masking active on counter registers.
