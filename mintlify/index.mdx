DRX is a privacy-first operating system built for retail store operators, managers, and staff who need clear, straightforward answers during live store hours.

Use this documentation hub to run DRX smoothly across your desktop browser, iPad, iPhone, and Android mobile devices.

## What this documentation covers

- Opening a store, business onboarding, and register checks
- Ringing sales through standard Sales, Quick Sale, and weight-based fractional selling
- Handling delivery orders, dispatching couriers, and tracking drop-offs
- Receiving inventory shipments, stock adjustments, and audit trail records
- Managing customer profiles, loyalty point rewards, and store credit
- Running handheld mobile registers on iOS and Android devices with offline support
- Setting staff permissions, shift security, and customer privacy protections

## Tips for store teams

- Use the main navigation menu to jump between screens: **Quick Sale**, **Sales**, **Deliveries**, **Inventory**, **Customers**, **Credit**, **Operations Insights**, **Reports**, and **Settings**.
- If a feature is marked as a preview or beta, it is actively expanding; keep your store's standard manual fallback ready when needed.
- If you run into an unexpected error, report it directly through the in-app help menu and include the screen you were using.
