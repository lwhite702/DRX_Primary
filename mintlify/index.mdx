# DRX documentation

DRX is a privacy-first regulated-retail operating system built for operators who need clear answers during live store hours.

Use this documentation hub to run DRX day to day across web, iOS, and Android.

## What this documentation covers

- Shift-start setup, business onboarding, and register checks
- Standard Sales, Quick Sale, and fractional weight-based selling
- Delivery queue management, courier dispatch, and fee allocation
- Inventory intake, adjustments, and audit trail records
- Customer profiles, loyalty rewards, and store credit workflows
- Native iOS and Android handheld POS setup and offline sync
- Team roles, session controls, and privacy protections
