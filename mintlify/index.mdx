## Golden rules of the counter

- **Check before you double-tap**: If a slow Wi-Fi blip makes a sale or repayment hesitate, look up the order in **Sales History** before swiping a card a second time.
- **Separate store tabs from sales**: When regulars come in to pay down their tab, record it under **Credit Repayment** so your end-of-day register drawer balances to the exact penny.
- **Protect customer privacy**: Never scribble real phone numbers or full names on public counters or in support tickets. DRX's alias system exists to keep your patrons safe.
