## When something does not look right

Read the message on screen and check the saved record before repeating an action. This matters most for sales, payments, customer balances, Inventory counts, privacy settings, and account access.

Start with [Help and support](/start/help-and-support) when you are unsure which guide applies. Keep private customer and business information out of public feedback posts.
