This guide explains how to invite staff members to your store, assign job roles, and manage register permissions in DRX.

## What this page is for

Use this guide to assign appropriate staff permissions (Cashier, Supervisor, Manager, Owner), protect sensitive store settings, and prevent accidental data errors caused by over-permissioned accounts.

Manage your staff permissions from **Settings** -> **Manage Team** (`/settings/team`).

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Store Owner or Manager with team management permissions.
- **Key screen**: **Manage Team** (`/settings/team`).
- **Staff role overview**:
  - **Cashier**: Ring sales, scan barcodes, accept payments, search customers, and apply basic promotions.
  - **Supervisor**: Cashier abilities plus manual discounts, order voids, price overrides, and customer credit reviews.
  - **Manager**: Full store operations including inventory intake, manual stock adjustments, team invites, and store profile editing.
  - **Owner**: Full access across all stores, billing, and system controls.

## Fast path

1. **Open Team Management** (`/settings/team`):
   - Review your current list of active staff members.
2. **Invite new staff member**:
   - Tap **Invite Team Member**.
   - Enter their work email address or mobile phone number.
   - Choose their starting role (e.g. Cashier).
3. **Save and send invitation**:
   - The team member receives an invite link to create their password and set up their PIN.
4. **Change or update roles as responsibilities grow**:
   - When promoting a staff member to shift lead, tap their profile and update their role to Supervisor.
5. **Remove or deactivate former staff**:
   - When a team member leaves your store, deactivate their account immediately to revoke register access and sign out active mobile devices.

## Role permission matrix

| Store Task | Cashier | Supervisor | Manager | Store Owner |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Ring sales & scan items | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Accept payments & print receipts | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Approve voids & price overrides | ❌ No | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Look up customer purchase history | View Only | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Receive stock & log adjustments | ❌ No | ❌ No | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Edit store tax & receipt settings | ❌ No | ❌ No | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Invite staff & manage team roles | ❌ No | ❌ No | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Account billing & store ownership | ❌ No | ❌ No | ❌ No | ✅ Yes |

## If blocked

- **"Manage Team" screen is greyed out or view-only**:
  - Your account is a Cashier or Supervisor profile. Only Managers and Store Owners can manage team permissions.
- **Invited team member did not receive invite**:
  - Double-check the email address for typos and ask them to check their spam or promotions folder.
  - Tap **Resend Invitation** in the team list.
- **Owner account cannot be modified**:
  - This is an intentional security safeguard. Store ownership cannot be reassigned from a standard staff screen.
- **Staff member cannot perform an action they need**:
  - Check their assigned role in the matrix above and upgrade their role if their daily duties require additional permissions.

## Store security recommendations

- Give each staff member their own individual login rather than sharing a single storewide cashier password.
- Review your active team list on the first of every month to remove seasonal or former employees.
- Keep the Store Owner login secure and use a Manager account for everyday store operations.
