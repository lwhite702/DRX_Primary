# Team access

Deelrx CRM controls access by role through **Team Management** and permission checks across the app.

## What this does

- Shows current team members and roles
- Lets authorized users change role assignments
- Gates pages and actions by permission

## Why this matters

Role hygiene prevents avoidable inventory, sales, and settings errors.

## Step-by-step

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Click **Manage Team**.
3. Review each role assignment.
4. Update role where needed and confirm.
5. Re-test restricted actions with that user account.

## Common mistakes

- Granting broad roles for convenience and forgetting to roll back
- Assuming all supervisors/managers can perform owner-level actions
- Not validating role impact on live workflows

## Edge cases

- **Owner** role is protected and not freely reassigned in the same flow.
- Team access UI is visible, but actions are blocked without manage permission.

## Current limitations

- Team invite lifecycle is not fully surfaced in this page flow.
