This guide explains how DRX handles weight-based fractional sales, dollar-amount requests, bulk price breaks, and loyalty rewards at checkout.

## What this page is for

Use this guide to set up and sell items measured by weight (such as loose goods sold by grams, ounces, or pounds) and configure volume price breaks for customers buying in larger quantities.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Cashier, supervisor, or manager.
- **Key screens**:
  - **Products** (`/inventory`): Set up base prices and volume discount tiers.
  - **New sale** (`/pos/sale`): Ring up weight-based sales and tap dollar presets.
- **Supported units of measurement**: Grams (`g`), Ounces (`oz`), Pounds (`lb`), Kilograms (`kg`), and individual counts (`each`).

## Fast path

1. **Enter exact measured weight**:
   - In your POS shopping cart, tap the weight or quantity field next to the product.
   - Enter the scale reading (e.g. `3.5g` or `0.25 oz`).
   - The total price calculates automatically based on your item's price per unit.
2. **Use dollar amount presets ($10, $20, $35, $50)**:
   - When a customer says _"Give me \$20 worth of this item"_, tap the **\$20** quick button or type `$20.00`.
   - DRX calculates the exact weight down to 3 decimal places without rounding errors.
   - The cart updates both the target weight to weigh on your scale and the exact \$20 total.
3. **Automated bulk tier discounts**:
   - Volume pricing applies automatically as customer quantities cross key thresholds:
     - **Eighth (3.5g)**: Tier 1 rate
     - **Quarter (7.0g)**: Tier 2 rate
     - **Half Ounce (14.0g)**: Tier 3 rate
     - **Full Ounce (28.0g)**: Tier 4 rate
4. **Redeem customer loyalty rewards**:
   - If an attached customer has earned loyalty points, tap the **Loyalty Reward** button at checkout to deduct their discount before sales tax is applied.

## If blocked

- **Dollar amount button gives an unexpected weight calculation**:
  - Open the product in **Products** and verify the base price per gram or per ounce.
- **Bulk price discount is not applying**:
  - Check that the cart weight meets or exceeds the required tier threshold (e.g. 7.0g for a quarter).
- **Loyalty reward button cannot be clicked**:
  - Ensure the customer profile is attached to the cart and confirm they have an active points balance.
