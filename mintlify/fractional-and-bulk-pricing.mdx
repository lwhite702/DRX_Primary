This guide explains how DRX handles weight-based fractional selling, reciprocal dollar inputs, bulk price breaks, and loyalty rewards at checkout.

## What this page is for

Use this guide to configure and operate fractional products (such as loose goods sold by grams, fractions of an ounce, or custom weights) and understand automated bulk tier discounts.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Cashier, supervisor, or manager.
- **Relevant routes**:
  - `/inventory` (configure base price and bulk tiers)
  - `/pos/sale` (fractional and reciprocal checkout)
- **Supported units**: Grams (`g`), Ounces (`oz`), Pounds (`lb`), Kilograms (`kg`), and discrete units (`each`).

## Fast path

1. **Exact weight entry**:
   - In the POS cart, tap the product quantity or weight input.
   - Enter the exact measured weight (for example `3.5g` or `0.25 oz`).
   - Total price calculates automatically based on unit price and tier logic.
2. **Reciprocal dollar amount entry**:
   - When a customer requests a specific dollar amount (e.g., "$20 worth"), tap the dollar entry field or select a preset chip ($10, $20, $35, $50).
   - DRX calculates the corresponding exact weight to 3 decimal places without roundoff error.
   - The cart updates both weight and final line item cost simultaneously.
3. **Automated bulk price tier breaks**:
   - Products apply automatic volume price breaks when quantity thresholds are reached:
     - **Eighth (3.5g)**: Tier 1 rate
     - **Quarter (7.0g)**: Tier 2 rate
     - **Half Ounce (14.0g)**: Tier 3 rate
     - **Full Ounce (28.0g)**: Tier 4 rate
4. **Apply loyalty discounts**:
   - If the attached customer has available loyalty points, tap the Loyalty reward chip at checkout to apply discount credits against eligible items before final tax calculation.

## If blocked

- **Reciprocal dollar input generates unexpected weight**:
  - Confirm the base price per gram or ounce configured on the product in `/inventory`.
- **Bulk price tier not triggering**:
  - Verify that the item quantity meets or exceeds the minimum tier threshold.
- **Loyalty reward button disabled**:
  - Confirm customer profile is attached to the active cart and has positive reward balance.

## What is beta here

- Custom mixed-tier combination discounts across disparate product families are beta-limited.
- Standard weight-based fractional checkout, dollar reciprocal presets, single-product bulk tiers, and loyalty redemption are fully live.
