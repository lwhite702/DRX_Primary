# Fractional selling and bulk pricing

This guide explains how DRX handles weight-based fractional selling, reciprocal dollar inputs, bulk price breaks, and loyalty rewards at checkout.

## What this page is for

Use this guide to configure and operate fractional products (such as loose goods sold by grams, fractions of an ounce, or custom weights) and understand automated bulk tier discounts.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Cashier, supervisor, or manager.
- **Relevant routes**:
  - `/inventory` (configure base price and bulk tiers)
  - `/pos/sale` (fractional and reciprocal checkout)
- **Supported units**: Grams (`g`), Ounces (`oz`), Pounds (`lb`), Kilograms (`kg`), and discrete units (`each`).

## Fractional selling workflows

### 1. Exact weight entry
1. In POS cart, tap the product quantity or weight input.
2. Enter the exact measured weight (for example `3.5g` or `0.25 oz`).
3. Total price calculates automatically based on unit price and tier logic.

### 2. Reciprocal dollar amount entry
When a customer requests a specific dollar amount (e.g., "$20 worth"):
1. In POS cart, tap the dollar entry field or select a preset chip ($10, $20, $35, $50).
2. DRX calculates the corresponding exact weight to 3 decimal places without roundoff error.
3. The cart updates both weight and final line item cost simultaneously.

## Bulk price tiers

Products can be configured with automated price breaks based on quantity thresholds:
- **Eighth (3.5g)**: Tier 1 rate
- **Quarter (7.0g)**: Tier 2 rate
- **Half Ounce (14.0g)**: Tier 3 rate
- **Full Ounce (28.0g)**: Tier 4 rate

When a customer's cart reaches a tier threshold, the pricing engine applies the lower unit rate to the line item automatically.

## Loyalty discounts

If the attached customer has available loyalty points:
1. Tap the Loyalty reward chip at checkout.
2. The discount is applied against eligible items before final tax calculation.
3. Used points are deducted upon successful completion of the transaction.
