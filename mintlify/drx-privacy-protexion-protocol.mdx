DRX is built from the ground up with a **privacy-first design**. This guide explains how our built-in protections keep your customer information safe from prying eyes, data leaks, and unnecessary record hoarding.

## What this page is for

Use this guide to understand how customer privacy settings work, how to configure privacy levels for your store, and how to protect customer phone numbers on counter registers.

## Core privacy principles for store operators

1. **Collect only what you need**: Never ask a walk-in cash customer for their home address or phone number just to complete a basic sale.
2. **Alias-first design**: Customers can be saved using simple nicknames or initials (e.g. "Jay M.") without requiring full legal names.
3. **Screen masking at the counter**: Sensitive information (like phone numbers) is partially masked with stars so shoppers standing in line cannot read another customer's contact details off your screen.
4. **Automatic temporary profile cleanup**: Walk-in profiles can be set to automatically expire and delete after 1, 7, or 30 days of inactivity.

## How to configure your store privacy level

Open **Settings** (`/settings`) to choose the privacy profile that matches your store's workflow:

### 1. Alias Mode (Highest Privacy)
- **Best for**: Fast walk-in retail where customers value maximum discretion.
- **How it works**: Only the alias field is shown by default. Phone numbers and home addresses are hidden.
- **Customer experience**: Fast, private, and anonymous checkout.

### 2. Standard Mode (Balanced)
- **Best for**: Retail stores that offer digital receipt text messages and loyalty point rewards.
- **How it works**: Shows alias and an optional phone number field. Address and email remain optional.
- **Customer experience**: Quick loyalty point lookup with masked phone numbers.

### 3. Business CRM Mode (Full Contact)
- **Best for**: Operations that offer home delivery, invoicing, or commercial accounts.
- **How it works**: Displays full contact fields including delivery address and billing details.
- **Customer experience**: Complete profile with delivery tracking.

## Automatic data cleanup & expiration

You can prevent old customer lists from cluttering your store by configuring **Automatic Retention Cleanup**:

- **Temporary Walk-in Profiles**: Set to auto-delete after 1 day, 7 days, or 30 days of inactivity.
- **Active Credit Protection**: Customers who have an outstanding store credit balance or active delivery order are automatically protected from deletion until their balance is settled.

## Counter screen masking

When **Screen Masking** is enabled in Store Settings:

- Customer phone numbers appear as `(***) ***-5678`.
- Email addresses appear as `a***@example.com`.
- Cashiers can still verify customers by asking for the last 4 digits of their phone number.
- Managers can tap to reveal full details if needed for delivery or order resolution.
