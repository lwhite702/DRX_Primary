DRX lets you save fewer customer details, hide them on shared screens, and delete old records on a schedule.

## What this page is for

Use this guide to choose what customer details DRX saves and who can see them.

## The basics

1. **Collect only what you need**: Never ask a walk-in cash customer for their home address or phone number just to complete a basic sale.
2. **Use an alias when it is enough**: A customer can be saved with a nickname or initials. A full legal name is not required.
3. **Hide details on shared screens**: Phone numbers and other details can be covered when other people can see the screen.
4. **Delete temporary records**: Temporary profiles can be deleted after the time you choose.

## Choose what is saved

Open **Settings** (`/settings`) and choose a privacy setting:

### 1. Hide details

- **What it saves**: An alias only.
- **Old records**: Inactive customer records are deleted after 30 days.

### 2. Protect details

- **What it saves**: Contact details when you need them.
- **Old records**: Inactive customer records are deleted after 180 days.

### 3. Keep details

- **What it saves**: The full customer record.
- **Old records**: Inactive customer records are deleted after 365 days.

## Delete old records

Choose when DRX deletes temporary customer records:

- **Temporary profiles**: Choose the available deletion time in Settings.
- **Open balances and deliveries**: Check the current retention rule before deleting a customer record.

## Hide details on screen

When **Hide details** is on in Settings:

- Customer phone numbers appear as `(***) ***-5678`.
- Email addresses appear as `a***@example.com`.
- Cashiers can still verify customers by asking for the last 4 digits of their phone number.
- Managers can tap to reveal full details if needed for delivery or order resolution.
