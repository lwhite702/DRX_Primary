This guide explains how staff and managers use DRX on iOS and Android handheld devices for floor sales, fast barcode scanning, and delivery handoffs.

## What this page is for

Use this guide to set up mobile registers, ring sales while moving around the store, take orders when Wi-Fi drops, and receive driver arrival reminders.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Cashier, supervisor, or manager.
- **Supported devices**:
  - **Apple iOS**: iPhone and iPad devices running iOS 17 or newer.
  - **Android**: Android phones, tablets, and dedicated handheld barcode terminals.
- **Required device permissions**:
  - **Camera**: Needed for scanning product barcodes and customer IDs.
  - **Notifications**: Needed for courier arrival alerts and order reminders.
- **What to have ready**:
  - Your store sign-in email or phone number and password.
  - Your assigned store location or workspace name.

## Fast path

1. **Sign in to your account**:
   - Open the DRX app on your device.
   - Enter your login details and select your store workspace.
2. **Turn on quick unlock**:
   - Enable Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint unlock in Settings so you can wake and unlock the register instantly between customers.
3. **Ring sales on the floor**:
   - Tap **Quick Sale** to scan items with your device camera or pick products from the list.
   - Accept cash, credit card, or customer store credit.
4. **Keep taking sales if the internet drops**:
   - If your store Wi-Fi or cellular connection goes down, the app keeps ringing sales normally.
   - All offline orders save securely on the device and upload to your store records automatically as soon as connection returns.
5. **Receive courier arrival alerts**:
   - When a delivery driver is 2 minutes away, your device receives a pop-up alert.
   - Tap the alert to open the order details and hand off the package immediately.
6. **Switch users or locations cleanly**:
   - Tap your profile avatar in the dashboard header to check your active permissions, switch store locations, or sign out for shift change.

## If blocked

- **Device cannot scan barcodes**:
  - Check that camera access is turned on in your device settings.
  - Ensure the counter area has adequate lighting and hold the camera 4–6 inches from the barcode.
- **Face ID or fingerprint unlock fails**:
  - Enter your store passcode or PIN to unlock the screen.
- **Offline sales are not uploading after reconnecting**:
  - Tap the offline status banner or go to **Settings** and tap **Sync Now**.
  - Check that the device is connected to active Wi-Fi or cellular data.
- **Driver arrival alerts are not showing**:
  - Check that notifications are enabled for DRX in your phone or tablet settings.

## What is beta here

- Core mobile register checkout, camera barcode scanning, offline mode, quick biometric unlock, and driver reminders are fully live.
