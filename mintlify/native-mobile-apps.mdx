This guide covers installing, configuring, and operating DRX on iOS and Android native devices.

## What this page is for

Use this guide to set up native handheld terminals, enable offline sales queues, configure biometric lock, and manage background notification reminders.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Cashier, supervisor, or manager.
- **Supported platforms**:
  - **iOS Native**: Swift 6 and SwiftUI app with ClerkKit auth, Convex sync, and Apple Push Notifications.
  - **Android Mobile**: Expo / React Native app with encrypted SQLite local queue and biometric lock.
- **Required settings**:
  - Verified operator account credentials.
  - Camera permissions for barcode scanning.
  - Notification permissions for delivery courier reminders.

## Fast path

1. **Sign in to native app**:
   - Sign in with Clerk credentials or single sign-on.
   - Select your active business workspace.
2. **Enable biometric quick lock**:
   - Activate Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint unlock in app settings for rapid counter resumption.
3. **Ring sales on the floor**:
   - Use Quick POS to scan barcodes or search inventory.
   - Process cash, card, or store credit transactions.
4. **Operate offline during network drops**:
   - Transactions queue automatically in encrypted SQLite local storage.
   - When connection restores, queued sales synchronize automatically with backend audit trails.
5. **Manage courier deliveries**:
   - Receive background push notifications when couriers arrive (T-2 minutes).
   - Tap notification to open incoming delivery order for instant handoff.
6. **Switch workspaces or sign out cleanly**:
   - Tap operator avatar on dashboard to view active permissions or switch stores without session leakage.

## If blocked

- **Biometric unlock fails repeatedly**:
  - Use operator password or PIN fallback to regain register access.
- **Offline sales not syncing upon reconnection**:
  - Open "More" -> "Offline Control" and tap "Force Sync Queue".
  - Verify network signal strength before taking additional offline transactions.
- **Courier push notifications not delivering**:
  - Verify system notification permissions in iOS Settings or Android App Info.
