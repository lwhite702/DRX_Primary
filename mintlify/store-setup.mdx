Store setup is where you customize DRX for your store's specific brand, staff roles, tax requirements, and register rules.

## What this page is for

Use this guide to set up your receipt details, tax rates, cashier permissions, and customer privacy preferences so your registers run smoothly on opening day.

This guide explains what each setting controls and what to verify before your staff starts ringing customer sales.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Store Owner or Manager with Settings rights.
- **Key screens you will use**:
  - **Store Profile** (`/settings/profile`)
  - **General Settings** (`/settings`)
  - **Manage Team** (`/settings/team`)
  - **Product Aliases** (`/settings/inventory/aliases`)
- **Have ready**:
  - Official store name, phone number, email, and receipt address.
  - Your local sales tax decision and tax rate percentage.
  - Staff list and desired roles (Cashier, Supervisor, Manager).
  - Your store's manager PIN policy for approving discounts or voids.

## Fast path

1. **Set up your Store Profile** (`/settings/profile`):
   - Enter your store name, contact phone, customer support email, and physical address.
   - These details appear on customer receipts and store summaries.
2. **Configure Taxes and Register Controls** (`/settings`):
   - Turn the **Collect Tax** toggle on or off.
   - If on, enter your default sales tax percentage.
   - Choose whether sensitive register actions (such as manual discounts or voids) require a Manager PIN.
3. **Choose your Customer Privacy Profile** (`/settings`):
   - **Alias Mode**: Perfect for walk-in stores that only need a nickname or first name to track an order.
   - **Standard Mode**: Great for stores that collect optional phone numbers for loyalty or delivery.
   - **Business CRM Mode**: For operations that need full contact details.
   - Turn on **Counter Screen Masking** if you want customer phone numbers partially hidden from nearby shoppers.
4. **Set up Product Nicknames** (`/settings/inventory/aliases`):
   - Add slang, shorthand, or common customer nicknames to items so cashiers can find products instantly when searching.
5. **Invite Staff & Set Roles** (`/settings/team`):
   - Add your staff members and assign their roles (Cashier, Supervisor, or Manager).
   - Keep owner access protected.
6. **Verify staff experience**:
   - Sign in with a Cashier account to verify that restricted settings are properly hidden.
7. **Run a quick test sale**:
   - Ring up a sample item in **Quick Sale** to confirm that tax, line items, and receipts calculate as expected.

## If blocked

- **Cannot save changes in Settings**:
  - Your account may not have manager or admin permissions. Sign in with an Owner account to adjust storewide settings.
- **Team members cannot see invited stores**:
  - Confirm the email address was entered correctly and verify that the user accepted their invitation.
- **Tax is not calculating on sales**:
  - Go to **Settings** (`/settings`) and verify that the **Collect Tax** toggle is enabled and the tax rate is above zero.
- **Product nicknames are not appearing in search**:
  - Check the **Product Aliases** screen to ensure nicknames are active and mapped to the right item.
- **Customer details are hidden by stars or dots**:
  - Screen masking mode is active to protect customer privacy on the counter. You can temporarily view full details with appropriate manager permissions.
