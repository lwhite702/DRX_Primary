# Store setup

Store setup is where DRX becomes your store system instead of a blank shell.

## What this page is for

Use this page to configure business profile details, tax behavior, team entry controls, and privacy defaults so your first live week is stable.

This is the setup route map for owners and managers. It focuses on what each settings surface controls and what to verify before you let staff ring sales.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Owner, super admin, or manager with settings rights.
- **Routes you will use**:
  - `/settings`
  - `/settings/profile`
  - `/settings/team`
  - `/settings/inventory/aliases`
- **Have ready**:
  - Legal business name and store contact fields
  - Tax decision and tax rate
  - Role assignment plan for cashier/supervisor/manager
  - Manager PIN policy for sensitive POS actions
- **Reality check**: Setup values affect receipts, totals, and who can do what.

## Fast path

1. **Business profile** (`/settings` or `/settings/profile`).
   - Enter store name, phone, email, and address fields used in store records.
   - Save and re-open to confirm values persisted.
2. **Tax and POS controls** (`/settings`).
   - Set collect tax on/off.
   - Enter default tax rate if tax is on.
   - Set manager PIN behavior for controlled actions.
3. **Privacy profile and data handling** (`/settings`).
   - Choose alias-first or broader profile based on store policy.
   - Review field visibility, recommended fields, and screen masking mode.
   - Review ephemeral profile defaults and retention behavior.
4. **Inventory defaults** (`/settings/inventory/aliases`).
   - Verify alias mappings used by staff for fast lookup.
   - Remove duplicates before live shift starts.
5. **Team controls** (`/settings/team`).
   - Assign cashier, supervisor, and manager roles.
   - Confirm owner role remains protected.
6. **Sanity test**.
   - Sign in as one lower role account.
   - Verify expected denied actions are actually blocked.
7. **Live test flow**.
   - Add one inventory item, run one sale, then verify totals and inventory movement.

## If blocked

- **Cannot edit settings**:
  - Your role likely lacks settings/system scope.
  - Use owner/super admin for initial store setup.
- **Team screen opens but actions fail**:
  - Role can view but not manage users.
  - Confirm current user has user-management permissions.
- **Tax totals look wrong in checkout**:
  - Confirm collect-tax toggle and rate in settings.
  - Confirm the sale route is in the correct business context.
- **Inventory aliases not working in search**:
  - Check alias screen for duplicates and disabled entries.
  - Re-test from POS after saving.
- **Masking or privacy view differs from expectation**:
  - Recheck screen privacy mode and manual toggle behavior in settings.

## What is beta here

- Some expanded reporting and multi-business controls remain beta-limited.
- Core setup controls in `/settings` are live and should be treated as source of truth.
