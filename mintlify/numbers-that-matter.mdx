This guide explains how to read the live numbers on your **Dashboard** and **Operations Insights** screens to make smart decisions during your store shift.

## What this page is for

Use this guide at the beginning, middle, and end of your shift to monitor sales volume, keep an eye on low-stock items, track pending supplier intakes, and manage store credit exposure.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Cashier, Supervisor, Manager, or Store Owner.
- **Key screens**:
  - **Dashboard** (`/dashboard`): Real-time daily overview.
  - **Operations Insights** (`/insights`): Detailed sales metrics, alert counts, and recent intake logs.
  - **Inventory** (`/inventory`): Drill into specific low-stock products.
  - **Credit** (`/credit`): Review store credit exposure.
- **Workspace check**: Confirm your active store location name at the top of the screen to ensure metrics reflect the current physical store.

## Fast path

1. **Check the Dashboard at shift opening** (`/dashboard`):
   - Review yesterday's closing totals and ensure today's counters start fresh.
2. **Review live sales in Operations Insights** (`/insights`):
   - **Today's Gross Sales**: Total dollar revenue collected today across all registers.
   - **Transaction Count**: Total number of completed customer purchases today.
   - **Average Sale Value (Basket Size)**: Average spend per customer order.
3. **Monitor inventory alert cards**:
   - **Low-Stock Alerts**: Count of items currently at or below their reorder point. Tap to view items that need shelf restocking.
   - **Pending Intakes**: Supplier shipments received but not yet approved or added to active floor stock.
4. **Mid-shift volume check**:
   - If average sale value drops sharply or transactions slow down, check your product stock levels to ensure top sellers haven't run out.
5. **Shift closing review**:
   - Compare your end-of-day cash and card drawer receipts against the **Gross Sales** and **Tender Breakdown** numbers in Operations Insights.

## If blocked

- **All metrics show $0 despite customer traffic**:
  - Check that completed sales were properly closed out and not left open in draft carts.
  - Confirm you are viewing the current store location.
- **Low-stock alert count seems higher than actual shelves**:
  - Open **Inventory** to check if reorder thresholds on certain items were set higher than necessary.
  - Check if recent supplier deliveries are still waiting in the pending approval queue.
- **Pending intake shipments never clear**:
  - A manager needs to open **Receive Stock** (`/inventory/intake`) and tap **Approve Intake**.
- **Credit exposure metric is unusually high**:
  - Open **Credit** (`/credit`) to identify customers with overdue tabs or high outstanding balances.

## How different roles read shift numbers

- **Cashiers**: Keep an eye on the transaction counter and notify supervisors if a low-stock alert appears on a fast-selling item.
- **Supervisors**: Monitor pending intake shipments so incoming stock gets moved to shelves before peak hours.
- **Managers & Owners**: Track daily revenue targets, average ticket size, and outstanding credit exposure across shifts.
