This page explains how to read the live metrics in **Dashboard** and **Operations Insights**.

## What this page is for

Use this guide at shift start, mid-shift, and close to make quick decisions from current numbers instead of guesswork.

This page is focused on route-accurate operator reading, not deep analyst reporting.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Cashier, supervisor, manager, or owner based on route access.
- **Routes used**:
  - `/dashboard`
  - `/insights` (Operations Insights)
  - `/inventory`
  - `/credit`
- **Important check**:
  - Make sure you are in the right business/workspace before trusting totals.

## Fast path

1. **Start in Dashboard** (`/dashboard`).
   - Confirm day trend is not flat due to wrong context.
2. **Open Operations Insights** (`/insights`).
   - Review today sales total and transaction count.
   - Review average sale value.
   - Review inventory alerts and pending intake count.
3. **Check activity tiles**.
   - Recent intakes
   - Low-stock items
4. **Cross-check when needed**.
   - If alerts spike, open `/inventory` and `/inventory/intake`.
   - If credit exposure looks high, open `/credit`.
5. **Use reports route carefully**.
   - `/reports` exists, but output remains minimal in current build.

## If blocked

- **All zeros but store has activity**:
  - Confirm correct date and business context.
  - Confirm transactions actually completed.
- **Low-stock count seems wrong**:
  - Recheck reorder threshold on flagged products.
  - Confirm recent intakes posted or approved.
- **Pending intakes never drop**:
  - Intake may require approval; confirm queue path and status handling.
- **Credit risk seems high without clear cause**:
  - Check recent extensions and payments in `/credit` before escalating.

## Metric reading guardrails

- Treat sharp metric swings as a signal to validate route context first.
- Use inventory alerts as action triggers, not passive warnings.
- Use pending intake counts to avoid selling stock that is not yet posted.

## Shift-close review

- Confirm day sales count is plausible for lane traffic.
- Confirm no high-priority stock alert was ignored.
- Confirm credit exposure changes have owner/manager visibility.

## Role-based read order

- **Cashier**: transaction count, low-stock alerts that block lane flow.
- **Supervisor**: pending intakes and alert trend for current shift.
- **Manager/owner**: day sales, average sale drift, credit exposure movement.

## Escalation thresholds

Escalate same shift when:

- Inventory alerts jump sharply with no intake context.
- Average sale drops hard versus normal pattern and route checks look clean.
- Pending intake queue blocks expected stock for top sellers.

## Daily close note

Capture one short note each close with the top driver behind day totals. Over a week, these notes make trend review faster and reduce guesswork.
