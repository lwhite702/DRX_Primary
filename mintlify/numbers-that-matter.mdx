# Numbers that matter

Deelrx CRM gives you live operational metrics in **Operator Dashboard** and **Event Desk Board**.

## What this does

- Shows daily and recent operational signals
- Surfaces inventory alerts and pending intake counts
- Tracks customer and credit exposure metrics

## Why this matters

You can decide faster when the numbers are in one place.

## Step-by-step

1. Start each shift in **Operator Dashboard**.
2. Check sales activity and system health.
3. Open **Event Desk Board** for totals, averages, and alerts.
4. Use the metrics to prioritize restock, collections, and staffing.

## Common mistakes

- Treating zero values as bugs before confirming date scope
- Ignoring low-stock flags until checkout failures start
- Checking numbers without confirming current business context

## Edge cases

- Fresh stores can show sparse or zero metrics until activity exists.
- Real-time values can shift while you are actively processing sales.

## Current limitations

- **Reports** currently shows an empty state: "No reports yet".
