This guide is the fastest safe path from first sign-in to first completed sale.

## What this page is for

Use this page when you are opening a new store in DRX, setting up a fresh business profile, or handing DRX to a new manager who must verify the system is ready before staff begin taking orders.

This guide is not deep setup detail for every setting. It is a launch checklist with route-level links so you can get live quickly without skipping steps that break checkout later.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Owner, super admin, or manager with settings access.
- **Account access**: You can sign in and reach the app shell.
- **Business context**: Confirm you are in the correct business/workspace before entering data.
- **Required data on hand**:
  - Store name and contact details
  - Tax behavior decision (collect tax on/off and rate)
  - At least one product you can add to inventory
  - One test customer profile plan (alias-only or full profile)
- **Prereq routes**:
  - `/onboarding`
  - `/settings`
  - `/inventory`
  - `/sales` or `/pos/sale`

## Fast path

1. **Finish onboarding first** in `/onboarding`.
   - Complete business details.
   - Choose privacy profile based on what data your team actually needs.
2. **Open Settings** in `/settings`.
   - Confirm business profile fields used on receipts.
   - Set tax behavior and manager PIN policy.
3. **Add your first product** in `/inventory`.
   - Create at least one sellable item.
   - Confirm unit, price, and current stock are set.
4. **Run one intake check** in `/inventory/intake` if you are receiving stock now.
   - Enter cost and weight.
   - Confirm whether intake posts right away or goes to approval.
5. **Create one customer profile** in `/customers`.
   - Alias-only profiles are valid.
   - Contact details are optional unless your store policy requires them.
6. **Run a test sale**.
   - Standard flow: `/sales`.
   - Fast flow: `/pos/sale` -> `/pos/payment` -> `/pos/complete`.
7. **Verify outcome**.
   - Sale record appears in `/sales/[id]`.
   - Stock changed in `/inventory`.
   - If credit was used, balance changed in `/credit`.
8. **Check live metrics** in `/insights`.
   - Confirm totals and transaction count updated.

## If blocked

- **"No business found" appears on pages**:
  - Return to `/onboarding` and complete missing required fields.
- **Cannot access Settings or Team routes**:
  - Your role is too limited. Use an owner/super admin account for initial setup.
- **Checkout fails during first test sale**:
  - Confirm cart is not empty.
  - Confirm stock is above zero for each item.
  - Retry once before rebuilding cart to avoid duplicate transactions.
- **Inventory page shows items but checkout cannot sell them**:
  - Verify item status and quantity.
  - Confirm you are in the same business context in all pages.
- **Credit sale path blocked**:
  - Attach a customer and verify eligibility before selecting credit tender.

## What is beta here

- **Reports depth** is still beta-limited. The `/reports` page is present but minimal.
- Some multi-location or cross-business controls are beta surfaces. Keep store-level checks in your day-to-day routine until those surfaces expand.
