This guide is the fastest, safest path from your first sign-in to ringing up your first customer sale.

## What this page is for

Use this guide when opening a new store in DRX, setting up your business profile, or training a new manager to verify that the register is ready before staff begin taking customer orders.

This is a launch checklist to get your store up and running quickly without skipping settings that might slow down checkout later.

## Before you start

- **Recommended role**: Store Owner or Manager with Settings access.
- **Account access**: Ensure you can sign in to DRX on your browser or mobile device.
- **Store workspace**: Confirm your store name appears at the top of the screen before entering data.
- **Information to have on hand**:
  - Store name, address, phone number, and email.
  - Tax collection settings (tax enabled/disabled and your local tax rate).
  - Details for at least one initial product to stock in inventory.
  - Your customer privacy preference (alias-only walk-ins vs full contact profiles).
- **Key screens you will visit**:
  - **Onboarding** (`/onboarding`)
  - **Settings** (`/settings`)
  - **Inventory** (`/inventory`)
  - **Sales** (`/sales`) or **Quick Sale** (`/pos/sale`)

## Fast path

1. **Complete store onboarding**:
   - Fill in your business details on the **Onboarding** screen.
   - Choose the customer privacy profile that best fits how your store operates.
2. **Review your store settings**:
   - Go to **Settings** (`/settings`) and verify your receipt header details.
   - Turn on tax collection if applicable and enter your store tax percentage.
   - Set up your manager PIN requirement for sensitive counter actions.
3. **Add your first product**:
   - Open **Inventory** (`/inventory`) and create a sellable item.
   - Set its unit of measurement, retail selling price, and starting quantity.
4. **Log an inventory intake**:
   - If receiving a stock delivery, open **Receive Stock** (`/inventory/intake`).
   - Enter your invoice cost, received quantity, and confirm cost-per-unit.
5. **Create a customer profile**:
   - Open **Customers** (`/customers`) and create a profile.
   - Entering an alias or nickname is sufficient for most walk-in customers.
6. **Ring up a test sale**:
   - For fast counter checkout, tap **Quick Sale** (`/pos/sale`).
   - Add your product, tap **Pay**, and complete a test cash or card transaction.
7. **Verify your results**:
   - Check that the completed transaction appears in **Sales**.
   - Confirm stock decreased correctly in **Inventory**.
   - If store credit was used, check the updated customer balance in **Credit**.
8. **Check live numbers**:
   - Open **Operations Insights** (`/insights`) to confirm your sales total and transaction count updated in real time.

## If blocked

- **"No business found" appears on screen**:
  - Return to the **Onboarding** screen and complete all required store fields.
- **Cannot access Settings or Team screens**:
  - Your account permissions are restricted. Sign in with an Owner or Admin account for initial store setup.
- **Checkout button is disabled during test sale**:
  - Ensure you added at least one item to the shopping cart.
  - Confirm the item has positive available stock.
  - Tap once and wait for the confirmation screen rather than tapping repeatedly.
- **Product appears in inventory but cannot be added to cart**:
  - Open the product in **Inventory** and confirm its status is active with stock above zero.
  - Verify you are logged into the correct store workspace.
- **Cannot choose Store Credit as payment**:
  - Attach a customer profile to the cart first, then verify their credit eligibility.
