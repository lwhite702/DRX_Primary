# Getting started

Deelrx CRM gets your store live with onboarding, core settings, and your first workflows.

## What this does

- Gets your business profile in place
- Sets your defaults (units, privacy preset, quick quantities)
- Gives your team a clean starting point in **Operator Dashboard**

## Why this matters

Setup drives everything downstream: receipts, tax behavior, product units, and staff access.

## Step-by-step

1. Sign in and complete the setup wizard.
2. Fill **Business Details** and **Owner Contact**.
3. Set **Units**, **Quick Quantities**, and **Privacy Settings**.
4. Review and complete setup.
5. Open **Operator Dashboard** and confirm your store is active.
6. Add at least one product in **Inventory** and one profile in **Customers**.
7. Run a test sale in **Sales**.

## Common mistakes

- Skipping tax setup, then seeing unexpected totals at checkout
- Leaving product catalog empty, then hitting cart errors on first sale
- Skipping team role setup until after staff starts using DRX

## Edge cases

- If no business exists yet, DRX shows setup prompts instead of normal pages.
- If onboarding was closed early, reopen setup from app flow and finish all steps.

## Current limitations

- Onboarding defaults to one timezone at setup time; adjust later in **Settings** if needed.
