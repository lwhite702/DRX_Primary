# Legal disclaimer

Deelrx CRM is software used by licensed operators. This page is not legal advice.

## Lawful use only

- Use DRX only for lawful business activity in your jurisdiction.
- You are responsible for following local, state, and federal rules that apply to your store.

## Prohibited use

Do not use DRX to support or hide unlawful activity, including:

- Unlicensed sales or distribution
- Diversion or trafficking
- Fraudulent records
- Money laundering activity
- Operations outside applicable law

## Data and records

- DRX records are operational records, not legal rulings.
- If a regulator or court requires action, follow that direction first.

## Operator interpretation

- Do not treat app access as permission to bypass store policy.
- Keep role limits and audit trail habits in place at all times.
- Escalate uncertain legal scenarios to owner/legal counsel before action.

## Enforcement and access

- Access and feature availability can be limited or removed for policy violations.
- Suspected abuse should be reported through official support channels.
