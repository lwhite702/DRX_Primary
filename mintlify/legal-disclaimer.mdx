# Legal disclaimer

Deelrx CRM is software for licensed dispensary operations. DRX is not a legal shield, and this page is not legal advice.

## Lawful use only

- Use DRX only for lawful business activity in your jurisdiction.
- You are responsible for following local, state, and federal rules that apply to your operation.

## Prohibited use

Do not use DRX to support or conceal illegal activity, including:

- Unlicensed sales or distribution
- Diversion or trafficking
- Fraudulent records
- Money laundering activity
- Any operation outside applicable law

## Data and records

- Audit and transaction records in DRX are operational records, not legal determinations.
- If a regulator or legal authority requires action, follow their requirements first.

## Enforcement and access

- Access and feature availability can be limited or removed for policy violations.
- Suspected abuse should be reported through your official support channel.
