DRX is business management software used by licensed, legitimate retail operators. This documentation is provided for operational guidance only and does not constitute legal, tax, or accounting advice.

## Lawful commercial use only

- DRX must be used exclusively for lawful commercial activity within your applicable jurisdiction.
- Store owners and operators are responsible for knowing and adhering to all local, state, provincial, and national laws governing their specific business operations, tax collection, and customer records.

## Prohibited activities

DRX software and services may not be used in connection with:

- Unlicensed sales, illicit distribution, or contraband.
- Fraudulent transaction records or deliberate sales falsification.
- Money laundering or evasive financial activities.
- Any business activity conducted outside applicable commercial laws.

## Operational records

- Transaction receipts, stock adjustment logs, and financial summaries generated by DRX represent operational records recorded by your staff.
- Always consult your licensed legal or financial counsel when establishing store policies, employee procedures, and tax filings.
