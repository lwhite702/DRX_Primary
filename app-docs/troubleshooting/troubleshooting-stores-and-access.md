## Overview

This guide helps you resolve common issues with stores and access in DRX. Use it if you cannot switch locations, you see the wrong store’s data, or an action is blocked by permissions. Follow the steps to confirm you’re in the right place with the right access.

## Before you start

- You can sign in to your DRX workspace on web or mobile.
- You know the store (location) you should be working in.
- Your workspace admin can adjust your access if needed.

## You can’t switch to another store

This happens when the location list doesn’t show the store you expect, or switching does nothing.

1. **Confirm** you’re signed in to the correct DRX workspace. If your company has multiple workspaces, sign out and sign back in to the right one.
2. **Open** the location switcher in the app header or main menu and look for the store name. If you don’t see it, you likely don’t have access to that location.
   - Check for a label showing your current store. Use that control to view available locations.
3. **Retry** switching by selecting the store once and waiting for the screen to refresh. Avoid tapping multiple times.
4. **Refresh** the app screen. On web, reload the page. On mobile, close and reopen the app.
5. **Ask** your workspace admin to add you to that store if it still doesn’t appear.

## You see data from the wrong store

You might notice sales, inventory, or customers that don’t match the location you’re working in.

1. **Check** the current store shown in the app header or main menu. Make sure it matches where you’re physically working.
2. **Switch** to the correct store using the location switcher, then wait for the dashboard or report to reload.
3. **Reopen** the page you’re using (e.g., sales, inventory, customers, reports) to ensure it updates to the new location.
4. **Verify** filters on the page. Clear any location or date filters that hide the data you expect.
5. **Confirm** with a teammate in that store that recent activity exists. If data is truly missing, contact your workspace admin.

## You can’t ring a sale in the selected store

You may see an error or the payment screen won’t proceed when trying to check out.

1. **Confirm** the correct store is selected in the app header or main menu.
2. **Check** that the items are in stock for this store on the item screen. If not, choose in‑stock items for this location.
3. **Retry** the checkout from the cart screen. Remove and re-add one item to refresh totals.
4. **Ensure** your internet connection is stable. Reconnect and try again if needed.
5. **Ask** your workspace admin to confirm you have permission to make sales in this store if checkout remains blocked.

## You can’t adjust inventory or receive stock

Buttons to adjust stock or mark items received may be disabled or actions fail to save.

1. **Confirm** you are in the correct store. Inventory changes are per location.
2. **Reopen** the item or purchase order and try again.
3. **Check** if the item exists in this store’s catalog. If it doesn’t appear, ask your admin to add it to this location.
4. **Ask** your workspace admin to grant the required permission if controls stay disabled.

## You can’t view customers or edit customer details

Customer lists or profiles may be hidden, or edit controls may be unavailable.

1. **Verify** you’re in the intended store. Customer visibility can vary by location.
2. **Clear** any filters on the customer list (such as date or segment) that might hide results.
3. **Search** using a simple term (name, phone, or email fragment you’re allowed to store) and try again.
4. **Ask** your workspace admin to confirm your access if you still can’t view or edit customers.

## Reports don’t match what your team expects

Totals, margins, or counts may look off compared to what another user sees.

1. **Check** the report’s store selector and date range. Make sure both are set the same as your teammate’s.
2. **Refresh** the report after switching stores or dates and wait for it to finish loading.
3. **Compare** filters (such as product groups or staff) and clear them if you’re unsure.
4. **Confirm** recent sales were completed in the same store and on the same day. Reconcile with your teammate if differences remain.
5. **Ask** your workspace admin to review your access if some report sections are hidden.

## You don’t have permission to perform an action

You may see a message that an action is blocked or a control is disabled.

1. **Read** the message to confirm which action is restricted (for example, editing inventory, viewing reports, or applying refunds).
2. **Confirm** you’re in the correct store. Some permissions differ per location.
3. **Try** a read-only action in the same area to ensure the page is loading correctly.
4. **Contact** your workspace admin and request the specific access for the action in this store.

## Still need help

- Recheck your current store, filters, and date ranges before trying again.
- If issues persist, contact your workspace admin and share the store name, the screen you were on, and the time the issue occurred.
