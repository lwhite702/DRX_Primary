## Overview

This guide helps you solve common customer and alias issues in DRX. You’ll learn how to create or find an alias-based profile, view purchase history tied to an alias, and attach the right customer during checkout without collecting extra personal data.

## Before you start

- You are signed in to DRX on desktop or mobile.
- You have permission to use customer features in your shop’s workspace.
- You understand that DRX supports alias-first profiles and that customer attachment is optional at checkout.

## I can’t create a customer without real personal details

You hit this when you want to track a customer using only an alias, but you feel forced to add phone, email, or other contact info.

1. **Create** a new customer using only an alias.
   - Enter a nickname or initials in the alias field.
   - Leave contact fields blank if the customer does not consent to sharing them.
2. **Save** the profile as-is.
   - DRX supports alias-first profiles; contact fields are optional.
3. **Confirm** the profile shows only the alias.
   - You should see the alias listed without any extra personal details.

## I can’t find an existing alias when searching customers

This occurs when the search returns no results for a customer you know exists under an alias.

1. **Search** by the exact alias spelling and spacing.
   - Try common variations the customer may use (e.g., with/without punctuation or numbers).
2. **Filter** your view to show all customers, not just recently used ones.
   - Broaden the search scope if a recent list is limiting results.
3. **Create** a new alias profile only if you are sure it does not already exist.
   - Ask the customer which alias they prefer to avoid duplicates.

## I see duplicate customers with similar aliases

You notice two or more profiles that likely represent the same person using slightly different aliases.

1. **Confirm** which alias the customer prefers.
   - Ask at checkout or during a visit.
2. **Attach** the preferred alias to the current sale.
   - Use that profile going forward to keep history in one place.
3. **Stop** creating additional aliases for the same customer.
   - Keep using the confirmed alias to prevent future duplicates.

## I can’t view a customer’s purchase history

You expect to see past purchases but the history looks empty or incomplete.

1. **Open** the correct alias profile.
   - Verify you selected the exact alias used on past sales.
2. **Check** that past transactions were attached to that alias.
   - If sales were rung up without a customer attached, they will not appear in the alias history.
3. **Use** the same alias consistently in future sales.
   - Attaching the same alias each time keeps the history accurate.

## I can’t attach a customer during checkout

At the point of sale, you want to link a sale to a customer but don’t see or can’t pick the correct alias.

1. **Search** for the alias before finalizing the sale.
   - Look up the customer by their alias; avoid entering personal data unless the customer offers it.
2. **Select** the correct existing alias.
   - If nothing matches and the customer wants tracking, proceed to create a new alias profile.
3. **Complete** the sale with or without a customer attached.
   - DRX allows transactions without attaching a profile when the customer declines.

## I’m being asked for data the customer doesn’t want to share

You want to respect privacy but think you must fill contact fields to proceed.

1. **Leave** optional contact fields blank.
   - Only the alias is needed to create or use a profile.
2. **Confirm** with the customer what they are comfortable sharing.
   - Add contact details only with consent.
3. **Proceed** with checkout without attaching a profile if the customer prefers no tracking.
   - Sales can be completed with no customer attached.

## Still need help

- Recheck the alias spelling and make sure you are viewing the correct profile.
- Confirm whether past sales were attached to an alias; unattached sales won’t appear in history.
- If you still can’t create, find, or attach an alias, contact your workspace admin for permissions or policy checks, or escalate through your shop’s support channel.
