---
title: "Troubleshoot AI Intelligence"
description: "Check unavailable or questionable beta suggestions without changing business records to force a result."
---

If an intelligence card won't load, displays an error, or makes a suggestion that sounds completely off the wall, don't worry. Here is how to troubleshoot smart suggestions without messing up your actual store records.

## 1. Analysis fails or the card won't load

1. **Check Beta Access**: Make sure your business has approved beta access to intelligence features.
2. **Check Your Connection**: Real-time sales analysis requires a stable internet connection. If your network temporarily dropped, refresh the page once.
3. **Never Fake Sales to Force Suggestions**: Do not ring up practice sales or alter product costs just to try to trigger an AI insight! Faking transactions will corrupt your real store margins and tax records.

## 2. A suggestion doesn't match your store's reality

- If a suggestion recommends reordering an item that you intentionally discontinued, simply ignore the suggestion. The system analyzes raw velocity numbers, but only *you* know your store's seasonal plans and supplier relationships.
- Always trust your physical shelf count and actual **Sales** history over any automated suggestion.

## 3. Register and checkout are completely separate

Rest easy: if the intelligence service is ever offline or slow to respond, your cash registers, barcode scanners, and checkout workflows continue operating at 100% full speed! An issue with background analysis never stops you from ringing up customers.
