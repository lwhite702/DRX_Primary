## Overview

This guide helps you resolve problems creating or finding customers, viewing customer history, and issuing or redeeming store credit in DRX. Use the sections below to identify your situation and follow the numbered steps to get back on track.

## Before you start

- You have access to your DRX workspace and can sign in.
- Your role allows you to view customers and process sales.
- You are working in the correct location if your business has multiple locations.

## You can’t create a new customer

You may hit this when adding a shopper at checkout or in the customer area and the customer doesn’t save or appears incomplete.

1. **Confirm** you’re in the correct location if your store operates in multiple locations.
2. **Enter** only the minimal details your store requires (for example, name or phone) to respect DRX’s privacy-first customer profiles.
   - If a field is optional, leave it blank rather than guessing.
3. **Check** for duplicate warnings. If DRX suggests a match, **select** the existing profile instead of creating a new one.
4. **Save** the customer and **wait** a moment for the profile to appear before repeating the action.
5. **Retry** creating the customer from both the checkout flow and the customer list if one path fails.

## You can’t find an existing customer

This happens when a customer exists but does not show up when you search during checkout or in the customer list.

1. **Verify** you’re searching in the correct location if your business uses separate locations.
2. **Search** using a different minimal detail the customer shared (for example, name fragment or phone digits).
3. **Remove** extra filters in the customer list (such as location or status) so the list shows all matches.
4. **Check** for alternate spellings or shortened names the customer may use.
5. **Create** a new customer only if you are sure no match exists to avoid duplicates.

## Customer history looks incomplete

You may see missing purchases or interactions in a customer profile, especially if sales happened at another location or under a duplicate profile.

1. **Confirm** you’re viewing the right location if your business operates across multiple locations.
2. **Search** for possible duplicate profiles for the same person (for example, a nickname and a full name).
3. **Review** recent sales to see if they were checked out without attaching a customer.
4. **Attach** the correct customer to the current sale so future history records properly.
5. **Note** any duplicates you find and share them with your workspace admin to consolidate records according to your store policy.

## You can’t issue store credit

This occurs when you try to grant store credit (for example, after a return or as a goodwill adjustment) and the credit does not save or apply to the customer.

1. **Select** the correct customer before issuing credit so DRX can attach the balance to that profile.
2. **Confirm** you’re in the correct location, since credit may be managed per location depending on store policy.
3. **Enter** the credit amount carefully and **save** the change.
4. **Check** the customer profile to **verify** the new credit balance appears.
5. **Retry** issuing the credit from the customer profile if it failed during checkout, or vice versa.

## Store credit won’t redeem at checkout

You may see a credit balance on the profile but cannot apply it to a sale.

1. **Attach** the customer to the sale before adding payment.
2. **Review** the items in the cart; some stores restrict credit on certain categories. If unsure, **ask** your workspace admin about store policy.
3. **Apply** the available store credit as a payment method and **confirm** the remaining balance is correct.
4. **Switch** to the correct location if the credit is scoped by location per your store policy.
5. **Complete** the sale and **check** the updated credit balance on the customer profile.

## You see duplicate customer profiles

This happens when the same person was added more than once with slightly different details.

1. **Search** for all variants of the customer’s name or phone to identify duplicates.
2. **Choose** the profile with the most complete and recent history to keep as primary.
3. **Follow** your store’s policy for merging or consolidating records. If you do not have permission, **escalate** the duplicates to your workspace admin with profile links.

## You don’t have permission to view or edit customers

You might see limited options or blocked actions when your role doesn’t allow customer changes or credit adjustments.

1. **Check** your signed-in account and **confirm** you’re using the correct user.
2. **Attempt** the action in a different area (customer profile versus checkout) to confirm it’s a permission issue.
3. **Contact** your workspace admin to request the required access for customer management or credit handling.

## Still need help

- Recheck that you’re in the correct location and the correct signed-in account.
- Gather details: what you tried, the customer name or phone (if available), and where in DRX you were working.
- If the issue persists, contact your workspace admin. They can review access, location settings, and store policies, and escalate to DRX support if needed.
