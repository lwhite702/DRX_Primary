## Overview

This guide helps you sign in to DRX, create your first store, add a few products, and complete a test checkout. Most teams finish the setup in under 15 minutes and are ready to take real payments and track inventory.

## What you need

- An active DRX account with access to create a store.
- Permission to manage products and sales in DRX.
- A web browser or the DRX mobile app with internet access.

## Steps

1. **Sign in**
   - Open DRX on the web or mobile and sign in with your account credentials. You land on your home area where you can manage stores, products, and sales.

2. **Create your first store**
   - Start a new store profile so DRX can track inventory, sales, and team access for that location. After saving, you have a store ready to add products and customers.

3. **Add products**
   - Create a few products with names, prices, and stock quantities so you can ring a sale and keep counts accurate. Each product appears in your product list and becomes available at checkout.

4. **Add a customer (optional)**
   - Create a customer record with minimal details (for example, a name) to keep your CRM privacy‑first while enabling receipts and visit history. The customer will be selectable during checkout.

5. **Open checkout**
   - Go to the checkout area for your store to begin a sale. You can scan, search, or select products to add them to the cart.

6. **Build the cart**
   - Add one or more products to the cart and adjust quantities if needed. The cart shows item totals and the sale total updates automatically.

7. **Select a customer (optional)**
   - Attach the customer you created to this sale to track purchase history. The customer link appears on the sale for reporting and future visits.

8. **Take payment**
   - Choose a payment method and complete the sale. When the payment is recorded, the sale is marked complete, and stock levels decrease for each item sold.

9. **Send or print a receipt**
   - Provide the customer with a receipt from the completed sale. The sale appears in your recent sales list and in simple business reports for the store.

10. **Review inventory and reports**
   - Check your product stock counts and look at basic sales reporting to confirm everything updated as expected.

## If something goes wrong

- If you can’t sign in, confirm you have an active DRX account and the correct credentials, then try again.
- If products don’t appear at checkout, make sure they were created for the correct store and have stock available.
- If stock doesn’t update after a sale, confirm the sale is marked complete and not voided or in progress.
