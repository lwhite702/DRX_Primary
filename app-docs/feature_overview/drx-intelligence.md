## Overview

DRX Intelligence helps managers spot issues and opportunities by surfacing AI suggestions drawn from your recent activity. You review each suggestion, check the related details in Sales, Inventory, and Credit, and then decide what to do. You can mark a suggestion as helpful or not, so future recommendations improve.

## When to use it

- Prioritize which products to restock or discount.
- Spot unusual sales patterns that may need action.
- Identify inventory discrepancies to audit.
- Review customer credit risks before approving orders or returns.

## How to use it

1. **Open DRX Intelligence.** From your DRX workspace, go to the Intelligence area to see your current suggestions.
2. **Choose a suggestion to review.** Select a suggested action (for example, a restock prompt or a pricing adjustment) to open its details.
3. **Read the rationale.** Review the summary explaining why the suggestion was surfaced (such as recent sales velocity, low on-hand units, or credit exposure).
4. **Verify in Sales.** Open Sales to confirm recent transactions, trends, or item performance related to the suggestion. Check date ranges, item variants, and any promotions that could affect demand.
5. **Verify in Inventory.** Open Inventory to confirm on-hand quantity, in-transit or reserved stock, recent adjustments, and any open audits that might affect counts.
6. **Verify in Credit.** Open Credit to review outstanding balances, customer credit status, payment history, and any limits relevant to the action.
7. **Decide your next step.** Based on what you verified, take the appropriate action in the relevant area (for example, create a purchase intake, adjust pricing, start an inventory audit, or update a customer’s credit decision).
8. **Record the outcome.** In the suggestion details, mark the item as completed or not relevant so your list stays current.
9. **Rate the suggestion.** Mark it as helpful or not helpful to improve future recommendations.

## Options and settings

- Suggestion details: View a short explanation and the data points the AI used to surface the item.
- Status controls: Mark a suggestion as completed or not relevant to keep your queue organized.
- Feedback controls: Rate a suggestion as helpful or not helpful.

## Tips

- Always confirm in Sales, Inventory, and Credit before acting; suggestions are starting points, not final decisions.
- If data looks off, check for recent inventory adjustments or pending audits that may temporarily affect counts.
- When in doubt, defer action and leave a note in your internal workflow so teammates know the suggestion was reviewed.
