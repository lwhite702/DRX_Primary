## Overview

Use DRX to keep inventory accurate from intake to audit. You can create new products, receive stock with the vendor and cost recorded, make adjustments with a clear reason, and review every change in the audit trail. While you work, you can also see which items are low on stock so you know what to reorder.

## When to use it

- You are adding a new product that has never been sold before.
- You received a shipment and need to increase on‑hand quantities with the right cost and vendor.
- You found a discrepancy and must adjust stock with a reason (damage, shrink, recount).
- You want to confirm who changed inventory and when, using the audit trail.
- You need to spot low‑stock items before they run out.

## How to use it

### Create a product

1. **Open your inventory**. Go to your inventory area from the DRX web or mobile app.
2. **Add a new product**. Start a new product entry.
3. **Enter product details**. Add the name, SKU or barcode, category, and any variants as needed.
4. **Set pricing**. Enter the retail price. You can add a default cost if you track average cost.
5. **Save** the product so it appears in your inventory list and is available at checkout.

### Receive stock with cost and vendor

1. **Open inventory** and locate the product you are receiving.
2. **Start a receive action** for that product.
3. **Enter quantity received**. Add the total units from the delivery.
4. **Add vendor**. Select the vendor you purchased from or enter a new vendor name.
5. **Enter unit cost**. Record the cost per unit on this receipt so reporting reflects the actual cost.
6. **Add reference details (optional)**. Include a note such as a PO number, invoice, or shipment reference.
7. **Save** to update on‑hand quantity and log the receipt in the audit trail.

### Make a reasoned adjustment

1. **Open inventory** and choose the product that needs correction.
2. **Start an adjustment**.
3. **Choose adjustment type**. Select increase or decrease based on your count.
4. **Enter quantity change**. Specify how many units to add or remove.
5. **Select a reason**. Choose a reason such as Count Correction, Damage, Loss/Shrink, Return to Vendor, or Transfer.
6. **Add a note (optional)**. Include context that helps future reviews understand the change.
7. **Save** to apply the adjustment and create an audit entry.

### Confirm changes in the audit trail

1. **Open the audit trail** for the product or inventory area.
2. **Filter by action**. View receipts and adjustments to see who made the change, when, and the quantity difference.
3. **Review details**. Confirm vendor, unit cost, reason, and notes match your paperwork.

### Check low‑stock visibility

1. **Open your inventory list**.
2. **Filter or sort to see low stock**. Use available filters or views that highlight items at or below their low‑stock level.
3. **Open the item** to review recent receipts and adjustments in the audit trail before reordering.

## Options and settings

- Low‑stock level: Set a per‑item threshold so the product appears in low‑stock views.
- Default cost: Store a typical unit cost on the product; you can override it when receiving.
- Vendor list: Keep vendor names consistent so you can filter receipts and audit entries by vendor.
- Notes: Add free‑text notes on receipts and adjustments to capture PO numbers, invoices, or reasons.

## Tips

- Receive stock as soon as it arrives so on‑hand quantities and costs stay accurate for sales and reporting.
- Always include a reason on adjustments; it makes the audit trail useful during recounts or audits.
- If counts look wrong, review recent sales and returns first, then check the audit trail for adjustments.
- Use low‑stock views before ordering to confirm recent receipts weren’t missed.
