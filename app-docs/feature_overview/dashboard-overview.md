## Overview

The DRX Dashboard gives managers a quick daily read on sales flow, low‑stock pressure, and operational health. You can see what’s moving, what’s at risk of selling out, and where operations need attention so you can decide what to act on first. Use it at the start of a shift or throughout the day to confirm pace and resolve issues before they impact customers.

## When to use it

- Check sales pace and confirm whether today is tracking above or below your usual flow.
- Spot inventory pressure from items that are low or trending to stockout.
- Review operational alerts that need attention (e.g., pending audits or adjustments).
- Decide what to act on first across sales, inventory, and operations.

## How to use it

1. **Open** the Dashboard from the main navigation in DRX.
2. **Scan** the sales summary to understand today’s sales flow. Look for today’s sales totals and any indicators of pace compared to a typical day.
3. **Review** low‑stock pressure. Identify items flagged as low or trending to stockout so you can plan intake, transfers, or substitutions.
4. **Check** operational alerts. Note any alerts related to inventory intake, adjustments, audits, credit, or payments that require attention.
5. **Prioritize** actions. Tackle items in this order: operational alerts that block selling, then low‑stock items with highest sales velocity, then other optimizations.
6. **Drill into** details. When a card or metric indicates an issue, open the related area (Sales History, Inventory, or Reports) to validate the data and confirm next steps.
7. **Assign** follow‑ups. Share the item list with your team or capture tasks in your standard workflow so ownership is clear.
8. **Recheck** the Dashboard after actions. Confirm that alerts clear and that sales pace and low‑stock pressure improve.

## Options and settings

- Filters: If available, apply workspace or date filters to focus on the location or day you manage.
- Refresh: If available, refresh the Dashboard to load the most recent activity before making decisions.

## Tips

- Start of day: Use the Dashboard to set priorities for the shift, then revisit mid‑shift to catch new pressure.
- Stockouts: If multiple items show pressure, act first on items with both low quantity and high recent sales.
- Audit trails: When resolving alerts, make changes from the related DRX areas so updates are tracked with role‑based access and audits.
