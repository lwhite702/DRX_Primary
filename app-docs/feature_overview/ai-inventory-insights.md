## Overview

AI Inventory & Pricing Intelligence in DRX helps you decide what to charge, how much stock to hold, and what needs attention. Once enabled for your account, you can view recommendations and forecasts on the Intelligence page and a dashboard widget. You can review details, take action on items, and give quick feedback so the system improves over time.

## When to use it

- See price recommendations for items that may be overpriced or underpriced.
- Check inventory forecasts to plan reorders and avoid stockouts or overstock.
- Review alerts that call out fast movers, slow movers, or unusual trends.
- Scan a quick summary on the dashboard, then open full details when needed.

## How to use it

1. **Open** the Intelligence page from your DRX operator dashboard to see AI insights once your account has this feature enabled.
2. **Review** the dashboard widget to scan top recommendations and alerts at a glance. Use it to spot what needs attention today.
3. **Select** an item or alert on the Intelligence page to open detailed insight. You’ll see the suggested action (for example, a pricing recommendation or a reorder forecast) and supporting context.
4. **Compare** the recommendation with your current price or stock position. Check the rationale and any projected impact shown alongside the suggestion.
5. **Take action** on the item in your normal pricing or inventory workflow. Use the recommendation as guidance, then update the item where you usually edit prices or purchase quantities in DRX.
6. **Acknowledge or dismiss** an alert after you’ve handled it so your dashboard highlights new or outstanding items next time.
7. **Rate** the recommendation or **leave feedback** in the insight view when prompted. Short ratings help improve future suggestions.

## Options and settings

- Intelligence page: View a list of pricing recommendations, inventory forecasts, and alerts with item‑level details.
- Dashboard widget: Quick snapshot of top insights; use it to jump into the Intelligence page for full context.
- Feedback controls: Simple rating or comment prompts on insights to refine future results.

## Tips

- If you don’t see the Intelligence page or widget, your account may not have AI Inventory & Pricing Intelligence enabled yet.
- Treat recommendations as guidance. Always confirm against your store’s pricing rules, supplier terms, and seasonal factors before changing prices or ordering stock.
- Dismissing handled alerts keeps the widget focused on what’s next and reduces repeat reminders.
