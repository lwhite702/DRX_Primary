## Overview

Use DRX across multiple store locations and switch between them so you only see the sales, inventory, customers, and reports for the location you’re working in. You can also change your active role (Admin, Manager, or Operator) if you have more than one permission level on your account. This helps you move quickly between stores, view separate data, and know exactly what actions you can take.

## When to use it

- Work at more than one location and need to see that store’s sales and stock.
- Compare performance by switching locations without logging out.
- Take actions that require different permission levels (Admin, Manager, Operator).
- Reduce mistakes by confirming you’re in the correct store before ringing sales or editing inventory.

## How to use it

1. **Open DRX** on web or mobile and sign in to your account.
2. **Confirm your current store** by checking the store name shown in the top area of the app (or the main header on mobile). This is the location your data and actions apply to.
3. **Switch stores** by opening the store selector in the header area and choosing the location you want to work in. DRX updates the view so sales, inventory, customers, and reports reflect the selected store.
4. **Confirm the switch** by checking the updated store name before you continue.
5. If you have multiple roles, **open your role menu** from your account area and **select the role** you want to use (Admin, Manager, or Operator). Your available actions and settings will adjust to your selected role.
6. **Return to your task** (ring a sale, adjust inventory, view customers, or open reports). All actions now apply to the active store and role.
7. When finished, **switch back** to your usual store and role using the same menus.

### What each role can do

- Admin: Full access across locations you’re assigned to. Can manage team access, view and edit inventory, customers, and reports, and configure settings for the business.
- Manager: Day-to-day control for assigned locations. Can run sales, manage inventory counts and pricing, view location reports, and oversee team activity for their stores.
- Operator: Frontline selling and basic stock actions. Can ring sales and view limited product and customer info needed for checkout.

## Options and settings

- Store selector: Choose the active location so all views and actions apply to that store’s data.
- Role selector: Pick Admin, Manager, or Operator if your account has more than one role.
- Current context indicator: The app header shows the active store and role so you always know what you’re working in.

## Tips

- Always verify the active store before ringing a sale or updating stock to keep records accurate.
- If you don’t see a store, your account may not be assigned to it. Ask an Admin to add you to that location.
- If a button or page is missing, switch to a role with the needed permission or contact an Admin.
- Reports and inventory views are location-specific; switch stores to see another location’s data.
