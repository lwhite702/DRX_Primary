## Overview

Inventory Audit Trail helps you trace how a product’s stock changed over time. It brings together intake, adjustments, and recent sales so you can explain unexpected deltas. Store managers, inventory leads, and cashiers with access to inventory can use it to investigate discrepancies and confirm that counts match activity.

## When to use it

- You see a product’s on-hand count change unexpectedly.
- You need to confirm whether a decrease came from sales or from an adjustment.
- You’re reviewing recent intake to verify that received quantities posted correctly.
- You’re preparing for or reconciling a physical inventory audit.

## How to use it

1. **Open** DRX and go to Inventory.
2. **Select** Audit to open the inventory audit trail.
3. **Find** the product you want to investigate. Use the search or filters available on the Audit view to locate the item.
4. **Review** the movement history for that product. You will see entries grouped by type: intake, adjustments, and sales.
5. **Compare** the “before” and “after” quantities on each entry to understand how the on-hand count changed.
6. **Identify** the event that caused the unexpected delta by looking at the date, quantity change, and movement type.
7. **Drill in** to recent sales entries (when available) to confirm the sale quantity aligns with receipts and current stock.
8. **Note** any adjustments and who performed them to follow up if something looks incorrect.
9. **Repeat** for other products as needed until the discrepancy is explained.

## Options and settings

- Filters: Narrow the audit list by product, date range, or movement type (intake, adjustments, sales) to focus your review.
- Sorting: Sort by newest first or oldest first to match how you prefer to trace events.

## Tips

- Start with the most recent entries and work backward; unexpected deltas are often explained by the last few movements.
- If a product shows no sales but stock decreased, look for an adjustment entry to explain the change.
- If intake appears but on-hand is still low, check for immediate sales or adjustments that followed the intake.
- Ensure you have the necessary role-based access to view audit details for all relevant products.
