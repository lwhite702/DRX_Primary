## Overview

Use Inventory Intake and Adjustments to keep your on‑hand counts accurate in DRX. You can record new stock as it arrives, capture total costs, and correct existing counts with precise increase, decrease, or set‑to actions. Each change requires a reason and is saved to the audit trail; some changes may require approval based on your team’s settings.

## When to use it

- Receive incoming products and record their total cost.
- Correct inaccurate counts so point‑of‑sale stock matches reality.
- Set an item to a known on‑hand quantity after a count or audit.
- Document why a change happened and, when required, route it for approval.

## How to use it

1. **Open** the inventory area where you manage stock.
2. **Find and select** the product you want to update. Confirm you’re working in the correct workspace.
3. **Choose** the action you need:
   - Increase (add stock)
   - Decrease (remove stock)
   - Set to (replace current count with a specific number)

### Receive incoming stock with cost (Increase)

4. **Select** Increase to record an intake.
5. **Enter** Received Amount/Weight. Use the same unit your product tracks.
6. **Enter** Total Cost for the received stock. This records the value of the intake.
7. (Optional) **Add** Supplier and Delivery Cost if you track them.
8. **Select** a Reason. Reasons are required for traceability.
9. **Review** the new on‑hand preview and cost details.
10. **Submit** the change. If approval is required, your submission will be sent for approval and applied after it’s approved.

### Correct counts (Decrease)

11. **Select** Decrease to remove stock.
12. **Enter** the Amount/Weight to remove.
13. **Select** a Reason to document the correction.
14. **Review** the updated on‑hand preview.
15. **Submit** the change. If approval is required, the change is queued until approved.

### Set to a known quantity (Set to)

16. **Select** Set to to replace the current count.
17. **Enter** the New Quantity/Weight you verified.
18. **Select** a Reason to explain why the number changed.
19. **Review** the before/after counts.
20. **Submit** the change. The system applies it after approval if your policy requires one.

## Options and settings

- Increase, Decrease, Set to: Three ways to adjust stock depending on whether you add, remove, or replace a count.
- Reason (required): A mandatory selection that records why the change happened and keeps the audit trail complete.
- Received Amount/Weight: The quantity of stock you are adding during intake.
- Total Cost: The cost for the entire intake, recorded with the transaction.
- Supplier (optional): Who provided the stock, if you track it.
- Delivery Cost (optional): Additional receiving cost, if applicable.
- Approval (optional, policy‑based): Some adjustments are applied only after an authorized approver reviews and accepts them.

## Tips

- Use Set to after a physical count to match DRX to your verified number in one step.
- Enter Total Cost on every intake to keep valuation and reports accurate.
- Always provide a clear Reason—this speeds up approvals and keeps your audit trail useful.
- If an adjustment doesn’t appear immediately, check whether it’s awaiting approval.
