## Overview

AI Intelligence in DRX gives you suggested prices, stockout forecasts, restock quantities, and simple demand trends. You review each suggestion, then apply it as-is or override it with your own choice and a reason. Every action is recorded in an audit trail. These suggestions never block checkout—your register keeps running while you manage pricing and inventory.

## When to use it

- You want quick, data-backed price suggestions for items.
- You see low stock and need help deciding what to reorder and how much.
- You want to catch upcoming stockouts before they happen.
- You need a clear record of why a price or restock decision was made.

## How to use it

1. **Open** AI Intelligence from your DRX menu to view current recommendations for pricing and inventory.
2. **Scan** the list to see each item’s “Suggested Price,” “Stockout Risk,” “Restock Quantity,” and any “Demand Trend” notes.
3. **Select** an item to open its details and read the rationale behind the suggestion.
4. To accept pricing, **click** “Apply Suggested Price.”
5. To override pricing, **click** “Override Price,” **enter** your price, and **add** a short reason (for example, competitor promo or seasonal markdown). **Save** to record the change in the audit trail.
6. To accept inventory guidance, **click** “Apply Restock Quantity.” This records the quantity and reason from the suggestion.
7. To override inventory, **click** “Override Restock,” **enter** your quantity, and **add** a reason (for example, supplier lead time or storage limits). **Save** to record the decision.
8. **Review** the audit note shown for your action to confirm it captured the user, time, and reason.
9. **Repeat** for other items, or **filter** the list to focus on high “Stockout Risk” items first.
10. **Return** to checkout at any time—recommendations do not pause sales.

## Options and settings

- Suggested Price: AI’s recommended selling price for the item.
- Stockout Risk: A forecast indicator that highlights items likely to run out soon.
- Restock Quantity: The quantity AI suggests adding to inventory.
- Demand Trend: A simple note (for example, rising or slowing) to give context to pricing and restock choices.
- Apply: Commits the suggestion as-is and logs it in the audit trail.
- Override: Lets you set your own value and reason; your choice is logged in the audit trail.

## Tips

- Add clear, specific reasons when you override—this makes the audit trail useful later.
- Tackle high “Stockout Risk” items first to prevent lost sales.
- You can accept pricing but override restock (or vice versa) per item—decisions are independent.
- If demand is rising, consider shorter review intervals so you can adjust faster.
