## Overview

DRX helps independent retailers ring up sales quickly, track inventory and purchase orders (beta), manage customers with minimal data, process refunds (beta), and view simple sales reports. This page walks you through the full workflow: checkout on mobile or desktop with scanning/search, customer attachment, discounts, and multiple payments; finding transactions and issuing refunds; maintaining customers with aliases, notes/tags (beta), and loyalty (beta); managing products, stock, batches, vendors, and adjustment logs; creating and receiving purchase orders (beta); and viewing/exporting basic reports.

## When to use it

- You need fast checkout with barcode scanning or search, discounts, and split payments.
- You want to attach or find customers using aliases, see purchase history, and apply loyalty (beta).
- You need accurate stock with SKUs/barcodes, batches, vendors, and a clear adjustment log.
- You create and receive purchase orders and reconcile quantities and costs (beta).
- You review daily/weekly/monthly sales and best‑sellers and export basic reports.

## How to use it

### A. Ring up a sale fast (mobile or desktop)

1. **Open** the checkout screen.
2. **Scan** a barcode or **Search** products by name or SKU to add items to the cart.
3. **Adjust** quantities in the cart if needed.
4. **Apply** a discount to an item or the whole sale when eligible.
5. (Optional) **Attach** a customer by searching their alias or creating a new alias‑first profile.
6. **Select** a payment method and, if needed, **Add** another payment to split across multiple types.
7. **Complete** the sale. The cart resets for the next customer.

### B. Refund a past transaction (beta)

1. **Open** transaction history and **Find** the past sale by date, customer alias, or receipt details.
2. **Select** the transaction and **Choose** items or enter an amount to refund.
3. **Select** the refund method to match your policy.
4. **Record** refund notes for audit tracking (beta) and **Confirm** the refund.

### C. Create or find a customer (alias‑first)

1. **Search** by alias to find an existing customer, or **Create** a new profile using an alias; contact fields are optional.
2. **View** purchase history to understand past orders.
3. **Add** notes or tags (beta) to capture preferences or important context.
4. (When enabled) **Apply** loyalty (beta) according to your program rules during checkout.

### D. Manage products and stock

1. **Add** products with a name, price, and optional SKU/barcode.
2. **Set** stock levels and **Adjust** quantities as needed; reasons are recorded in the adjustment log.
3. **Track** batches and vendors for each product to keep sourcing and expiry/lot details clear.
4. **Review** the adjustment log to see who changed stock, when, and the impact on costs and margins.

### E. Create and receive purchase orders (beta)

1. **Create** a purchase order and **Add** items by product, SKU, or barcode; include vendor and expected costs.
2. **Submit/Save** the purchase order to begin tracking.
3. When stock arrives, **Receive** items against the purchase order; **Enter** received quantities and actual costs.
4. **Reconcile** differences between ordered and received quantities or costs; updates flow into stock counts and cost basis (beta).

### F. View sales and export basic reports

1. **Open** the dashboard to **View** daily, weekly, or monthly sales totals.
2. **Check** best‑sellers and category performance to understand what’s moving.
3. **Export** basic reports to CSV for simple analysis.
4. Note: deep slices and downloadable advanced breakdowns are in beta and may be limited.

### G. Devices and access

1. **Use** the mobile‑friendly checkout on phones or tablets for in‑store flexibility.
2. **Log in** to the full desktop dashboard for inventory, purchase orders (beta), customer reviews, and reporting.
3. **Connect** supported barcode scanners and receipt printers for faster checkout.

## Options and settings

- Roles: Admin, Manager, and Operator access levels control who can perform actions like refunds and inventory adjustments.
- Locations: Separate store views with location‑based reporting and centralized admin access.
- Loyalty (beta): Optional program; when enabled, loyalty can be applied during checkout.

## Tips

- Use aliases to minimize personal data; attach a customer only when it benefits the sale or service.
- Keep barcodes and SKUs consistent to speed up scanning and reduce search errors.
- Record clear reasons in stock adjustments and refund notes to strengthen audit trails.
- When receiving purchase orders (beta), enter actual costs to keep margins accurate.
- If an item doesn’t scan, search by product name or SKU, then update its barcode later.
