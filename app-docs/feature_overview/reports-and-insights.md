## Overview

Reports in DRX help you see sales totals, trends, and operational signals over a specific date range. You can filter by dates, review sales and operations insights in one place, and compare report results with Sales to reconcile what was sold with what was recorded. This page walks you through running a date-filtered report and completing a basic reconciliation.

## When to use it

- Review sales totals and trends for a day, week, month, or custom dates
- Check operational signals to guide staffing, purchasing, or cleanup
- Reconcile report totals with Sales to confirm accuracy
- Share a clear snapshot of performance with your team

## How to use it

1. **Open** the Reports page in DRX.
2. **Set** your date range. Use the date controls to choose a preset period (for example, today or last 7 days) or select a custom start and end date.
3. **Run** the report to load sales totals, trends, and operations insights for the selected dates.
4. **Review** Sales totals. Note overall revenue, counts (such as number of transactions), and any visible trends.
5. **Review** operations insights. Look for signals that may affect your staffing, purchasing, or cleanup (for example, busy periods or low-stock patterns) within the selected dates.
6. **Open** Sales for comparison. Navigate to your Sales view for the same date range.
7. **Match** the date range in Sales to the exact dates you used in Reports.
8. **Compare** key totals side by side (for example, total sales amount and number of sales). Confirm that the figures align for the same period.
9. If something looks off, **adjust** the date range in either area to ensure both views cover the same start and end times, then compare again.
10. **Document** your reconciliation outcome (for example, note that totals match or record any differences for follow‑up).

## Options and settings

- Date range: Choose a preset period or select a custom start and end date to filter results.
- Sales totals and trends: View summed amounts and directional patterns for the selected dates.
- Operations insights: See signals that can inform staffing, purchasing, and cleanup decisions.

## Tips

- Always compare the same date range in both Reports and Sales before deciding there is a discrepancy.
- If results look unexpectedly low or high, widen the date range to confirm you are not excluding part of a busy period or closing time.
- Save a screenshot of both views when you complete reconciliation to keep a reference for audits.
