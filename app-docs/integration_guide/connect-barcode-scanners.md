## Overview

Connect a barcode scanner to DRX so you can scan products at checkout and during receiving. You can use a USB scanner on desktop, a Bluetooth scanner on desktop or mobile, or your device’s built-in camera for quick scans when a hardware scanner isn’t available.

After setup, you can scan to add items to a sale, adjust quantities, and update stock during receiving, with live inventory counts reflected in DRX.

## Before you start

- You have a DRX account with access to checkout and inventory features.
- Your products in DRX have barcodes or SKUs saved on their product records.
- You have one of the following ready:
  - A USB barcode scanner (configured to type scanned codes as text and send an Enter/Return after each scan).
  - A Bluetooth barcode scanner (paired to your computer, tablet, or phone as a keyboard).
  - A device with a working camera (for camera-based scanning in supported DRX screens).
- You can log in to DRX on the device you’ll use at the counter or in the stockroom.

## Connect

### A. USB scanner (desktop)

1. **Plug** the USB scanner into your computer.
2. **Open** DRX and **log in**.
3. **Go to** your checkout screen or the area where you enter or receive products.
4. **Click** into the item search or barcode field so the text cursor is active.
5. **Scan** a product barcode to confirm the scanner types the code and submits it.

### B. Bluetooth scanner (desktop or mobile)

1. **Turn on** your Bluetooth scanner and set it to pair mode (see the scanner’s guide).
2. On your device, **open** Bluetooth settings and **pair** the scanner so it appears as a connected keyboard.
3. **Open** DRX and **log in**.
4. **Go to** your checkout screen or the area where you enter or receive products.
5. **Tap or click** into the item search or barcode field.
6. **Scan** a product barcode to confirm DRX receives the code.

### C. Camera-based scanning (mobile or desktop with camera)

1. **Open** DRX and **log in** on a device with a camera.
2. **Go to** your checkout screen or the area where you enter or receive products.
3. **Select** the scan option to use your camera when it is shown on the screen.
4. **Allow** camera access if your browser or device asks for permission.
5. **Point** the camera at the barcode until you see the item appear.

## Configure

- Choose where you scan:
  - Checkout: adds items to the current sale, updates quantities, and applies discounts after items are added.
  - Inventory and receiving: adds items to a receiving list or adjusts stock counts where available.
- Set scanner behavior on the device:
  - Enable an Enter/Return suffix on the scanner so each scan submits automatically (refer to your scanner’s quick setup guide).
  - Set your device keyboard/input language to match the scanner, so numbers and symbols read correctly.
- Prepare products:
  - Ensure each product in DRX has a unique barcode or SKU saved so scans resolve to the correct item.

## Verify it works

- At checkout, place the cursor in the item search or barcode field and scan a known product. The product should appear in the sale and the quantity should increase if scanned again.
- In inventory or receiving, scan an item and confirm the stock or receiving line updates for that product.
- Scan an unrecognized barcode and confirm DRX prompts you to search or add the product before proceeding.
- Check live stock counts after a test receiving or sale to confirm inventory reflects the change.

## Disconnect or change access

- To stop using a USB scanner, unplug it from your computer and use search or camera-based scanning instead.
- To stop using a Bluetooth scanner, turn off the scanner and remove it from your device’s Bluetooth settings, then continue with another input method.
- To stop camera-based scanning, close the camera prompt in DRX or deny camera permission in your browser or device settings. You can return to typing or a hardware scanner at any time.
