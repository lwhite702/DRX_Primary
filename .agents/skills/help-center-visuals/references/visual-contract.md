# DRX Help Center visual contract

## Brand

- Background: `#0A0A0A`
- Surface: `#121212`
- Elevated surface: `#1A1A1A`
- Foreground: `#F5F5F5`
- Muted foreground: `#A3A3A3`
- Accent: `#CCFF00`
- Sans: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Mono: Geist Mono
- Radii: 6px controls, 10px cards, 14px panels

Use the lime accent as a locator or small focal point. Large lime fields overpower Help Center text and fail the calm tone.

## Safety

- Money, payments, missing balances, access, privacy, security, deletion, destructive actions, and failed transactions use literal visuals and no humor.
- Generated visuals may explain a concept but may not depict product UI.
- Screenshots use fictional data and must not be produced from customer or production records.
- An illustration may not imply availability, platform parity, payment processing, deletion recovery, anonymity, or a security guarantee.

## Accessibility

- Required information must also exist in article text.
- Alt text explains the useful content or action.
- Diagram text should meet 4.5:1 contrast and remain at least 16px after article-width scaling.
- Do not encode status only by color.

## Formats

- Header: 1600x640 WebP, target under 300 KB.
- Diagram: editable SVG plus 1400x800 PNG, target under 350 KB.
- Screenshot: cropped PNG, target under 700 KB.
