# DRX Brand Voice & Language Guide

## Core Purpose & Audience

DRX (formal: **Deelrx CRM**) is built for business that happens in person, moves fast, and depends on knowing the count.

We write for the **operator actually doing the work**: owner-operators, small crews, cash-heavy shops, and independent sellers where speed, privacy, and clear counts matter more than corporate process.

> **Internal Mantra**: *Keep the count right and the line moving.*  
> **Core Promise**: *Know what you have, what you sold, what you made, and who still owes you.*

---

## The Voice Attributes

The DRX voice is:
- **Casual**: Sounds like a real human who knows the work.
- **Direct**: Gets to the point without filler or throat-clearing.
- **Calm & Confident**: Doesn't oversell, exaggerate, or use hype.
- **Discreet**: Respects privacy and boundaries without drama.
- **Practical**: Explains what happened and what to do next.

---

## Sentence & Writing Rules

1. **Short, punchy sentences**: One idea per sentence whenever possible.
2. **Common, spoken words**: If you wouldn't say it aloud to a shop owner, don't write it.
3. **Use contractions naturally**: *It's, didn't, don't, you'll, that's*.
4. **Say what happened first**: State the fact, then give next steps.
5. **No corporate theater**: Drop the tech jargon, consultant talk, and Silicon Valley pitch deck speak.

---

## Vocabulary Standards

### Preferred Words
*track, know, see, move, handle, keep, front, owe, paid, missing, covered, clean, quiet, clear, count, stock, sale, money, tab, balance, customer, team, fix, done, saved, recorded, shelf, text, link, code.*

### Banned Jargon & Corporate Theater
Never use these terms in customer, marketing, or investor copy:
- **No SaaS clichés**: *streamline your operations, unlock growth, all-in-one platform, seamless integration, game-changing, revolutionize, leverage, synergy.*
- **No fintech/tech jargon**: *take-rates, peer-to-peer settlement rails, closed-loop fulfillment, asynchronous confirmation, immutable ledger, cryptographic primitives, robust infrastructure, scalable paradigm.*
- **No corporate buzzwords**: *omnichannel, enterprise energy, methodology, optimize, authenticate (use 'sign in' or 'check'), ecosystem.*
- **No unlawful or caricature phrases**: Never use *street-smart, street-level, run product, fast money moves, credit & fronts, who owes what, operate in the shadows, 0 data leaks, 100% uptime.*

---

## Writing for Investors

When explaining DRX to investors, apply the same brand voice:
- **Frame the operator's reality**: Describe actual daily friction (e.g. lost DMs, missed texts, double-selling stock, third-party delivery apps taking 30% cuts).
- **Explain the product simply**: What does the software do? *"Customer clicks a link, shop owner confirms the count, customer pays via Cash App or Zelle, order gets handed off."*
- **Explain the economics plainly**: Why does it matter? *"The operator keeps 100% of the sale, avoids payment holds, and keeps their customers. Every order updates their inventory and sales records, making DRX indispensable."*

---

## Naming Discipline

- **Deelrx CRM**: Full name. Use on first formal mention, legal pages, and app store listings.
- **DRX**: Operating name. Use inside the product, in buttons, badges, and day-to-day text.
- **Deelrx**: Permissible shorthand.
- **NEVER use**: `DRX CRM`.
