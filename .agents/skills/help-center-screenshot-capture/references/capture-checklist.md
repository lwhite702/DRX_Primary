# Screenshot capture checklist

## Before

- Confirm the documented platform and current route.
- Confirm release state and feature flag requirements.
- Compare visible labels with the feature canon and source.
- Load a fictional tenant or a reviewed fixture.

## During

- Use a deterministic viewport and time zone.
- Wait for fonts, data, and images.
- Disable animation and remove development overlays.
- Keep one primary action visible.
- Avoid hover-only states unless the step explicitly uses hover.

## After

- Inspect at full size for private data and wrong labels.
- Inspect at expected article width for readable text.
- Record whether the capture is verified product UI or illustrative fixture.
- Calculate the SHA-256 checksum and dimensions.
- Add alt text and, for fixtures, the required caption.
