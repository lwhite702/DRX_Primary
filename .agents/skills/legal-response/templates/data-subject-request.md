# DRX Legal Response Template: Data Subject Requests (DSR)

**Category**: Privacy & Data Protection  
**Applicability**: Retail customer or operator data access, deletion, or correction inquiries under CCPA/CPRA, GDPR, or state consumer privacy statutes.  
**Governing Entity**: Wrelik Brands LLC (DRX / Deelrx CRM)  
**Support Contact**: `support@deelrxcrm.com`  
**Last Reviewed**: October 2026  

---

### Use When
- A retail customer, former employee, or store operator submits an inquiry requesting access to their personal data, deletion of their profile, or correction of contact records.
- The inquiry comes through direct operator communication, store support, or `support@deelrxcrm.com`.

### Do NOT Use When (Escalation Triggers)
- **Litigation or HR Dispute**: Requester is an adverse party in an active legal claim, wage dispute, or arbitration.
- **Regulatory Authority**: Request is received directly from a state attorney general, privacy agency (e.g. CPPA), FTC, or foreign data protection authority.
- **Active Legal Hold**: The records are subject to an active litigation hold or preservation order.
- **Special Category or Minor Data**: Request explicitly claims the involvement of a minor's data or sensitive medical/biometric records.
- **Unprecedented Scope**: Request demands raw server backups, disaster recovery tapes, or third-party processor internal logs.

*When an escalation trigger occurs: Mark the inquiry as `ESCALATED - FOR COUNSEL REVIEW ONLY` and notify legal counsel.*

## Template 1: Acknowledgment & Identity Verification

**Subject**: `Data Subject Request Acknowledgment - Reference [{{request_id}}]`

```markdown
Dear {{requester_name}},

We have received your data {{request_type}} request dated {{request_date}} regarding records held within {{business_name}} / Deelrx CRM.

To protect the confidentiality of customer records and verify that personal data is never disclosed to unauthorized parties, we must confirm your identity before proceeding.

Please reply to this communication with:
1. The exact name, customer alias, phone number, or email address used at the store.
2. The approximate date and location of your recent store interaction or receipt reference.

Please note: DRX does not require, and you should NOT provide, government identification numbers (such as SSNs), unredacted banking passwords, or payment card security codes.

Once verified, we will process your request in accordance with applicable consumer privacy laws within 30 days (or 45 days if an extension notice is provided).

Sincerely,
Privacy Administration Team
Wrelik Brands LLC / Deelrx CRM
support@deelrxcrm.com
```

## Template 2: Deletion Fulfillment & Lawful Retention Boundaries

**Subject**: `Fulfillment of Data Deletion Request - Reference [{{request_id}}]`

```markdown
Dear {{requester_name}},

This notice confirms that in response to your verified data subject request dated {{request_date}}, {{business_name}} and Wrelik Brands LLC have processed your request across active customer management systems.

### Personal Information Actioned:
- Stored personal contact identifiers (including customer name, phone number, email address, and delivery notes) have been cleared and removed from your active profile.
- Operational and marketing messaging preferences have been deactivated.

### Statutory Retention & Operational Records:
In compliance with applicable commercial law, retail accounting standards, and tax reporting requirements:
- Completed sales receipts, tax remittance records, and transaction ledger entries are retained to satisfy statutory audit mandates. These financial ledger entries do not include marketing contact details.
- System backups and transaction logs rotate and overwrite in the ordinary course of technical operations.

If you have any questions or require additional details regarding our privacy controls, please consult our public Privacy Policy at https://deelrxcrm.com/legal/privacy or contact us at support@deelrxcrm.com.

Sincerely,
Privacy Administration Team
Wrelik Brands LLC
support@deelrxcrm.com
```
