# DRX Staff Manual

You are the operator. Keep the line moving and keep the records accurate.

## Start of shift (2 minutes)

1. Sign in and confirm workspace.
2. Confirm printer and scanner are ready.
3. Open Dashboard and skim top alerts.
4. Start with one task only: checkout, stock, or reports.
5. Review unresolved items from the prior shift.

## Checkout: step by step

### Start a sale

1. Open Quick Sale.
2. Add first item by scan or search.
3. Confirm item, price, and quantity.
4. Apply discount only if your store allows.
5. Choose tender(s), including split if needed.
6. Confirm balance is zero.
7. Complete sale and print or send receipt.

### Handle customer credit

1. Attach customer before any credit action.
2. Check outstanding balance.
3. Add a reference for credit extension or payment.
4. Confirm updated balance before finishing.

### Payment feels off

1. Recheck subtotal, tax, and total.
2. Confirm tender type is enabled for your location.
3. Validate each tender amount.
4. Retry once after reopening payment.
5. If still blocked, capture time/item/amount and send to a manager.

## Inventory flow

### Receive stock

1. Open product.
2. Start intake.
3. Enter received quantity and cost.
4. Add a short reason.
5. Save and confirm result.

### Correct stock

1. Choose Increase, Decrease, or Set to.
2. Enter quantity or target.
3. Add clear reason.
4. Save and verify status.

### Trace a mismatch

1. Open Inventory > Audit.
2. Confirm product, date, and workspace.
3. Compare before and after movement.
4. Match against sales in same window.

## End-of-shift close

1. Confirm all carts are complete.
2. Match report totals with Sales for your shift window.
3. Review pending adjustments and approvals.
4. Confirm receipts and credit actions are logged.
5. Leave one handoff item list.

## Common issues

### Barcode does not scan

1. Confirm product is active and searchable.
2. Scan one more time with fresh pass.
3. Re-pair scanner and test Enter behavior.
4. Use search if scan still misses.

### Receipt does not print

1. Confirm sale is marked complete.
2. Check paper and printer connection.
3. Reprint from sale detail.
4. Send digital copy as fallback.

### Stock moved too fast

1. Open recent sales and stock movements.
2. Check for pending approval.
3. Confirm returns, exchanges, or voids.
4. Bring to manager if no clear match.

## Supporting customers in person

Say these three lines:

1. “I hear you, we will fix this in one pass.”
2. “Tell me exactly where it happened.”
3. “I am checking the exact flow now.”

If you cannot close it on the floor, send to Customer Service playbook.
