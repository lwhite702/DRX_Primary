# DRX Admin Manual

You run the controls. Your goal is simple: keep the team moving and block bad changes before they affect customers.

## First 10 minutes

1. Open Dashboard.
2. Clear what you can from pending approvals.
3. Check role and team changes made since last shift.
4. Verify workspace settings for approvals and credit.
5. Run one quick checkout or stock flow to confirm live data.

## Workspace and role control

### Start every change with context

1. Confirm active workspace.
2. Confirm correct location.
3. Only change roles after you confirm the business need.
4. Reopen the user list and confirm the new role is active.

### Staff gets access denied

1. Confirm role supports requested action.
2. Confirm workspace membership and status.
3. Ask the staff member to refresh and re-sign in.
4. Log what changed and why.

## Settings you keep tight

- Require approval on high-risk stock movement.
- Keep credit authority narrow: who extends, who posts, who approves.
- Set approvers per workspace.
- Roll out feature changes one workspace at a time.
- Require reason fields on adjustments.

## Approval safety checks

1. Pull audit trail for the item or account.
2. Match it with sales and intake.
3. Ask for a clear reason if movement is unclear.
4. Approve only when evidence is clean.

## Incident response

1. Pause risky manual changes.
2. Ask for proof of the action.
3. Verify status after approval runs.
4. Write one short note:
5. What changed
6. Why it changed
7. Who changed it
8. Time and workspace

## When numbers do not match

1. Confirm report and sales are using the same workspace.
2. Confirm same date range and location.
3. Ask staff to refresh and re-run both views.

## When approvals stack up

1. Confirm approver role and workspace match.
2. Approve one item at a time.
3. Confirm applied status before moving on.
4. Escalate to owner if policy drift is the cause.

## Support handoff line

Use this exact line:

“I checked workspace and role. This is on policy hold. I sent it to the approver and will update you after it clears.”

Use it for access, credit, and report mismatches.
