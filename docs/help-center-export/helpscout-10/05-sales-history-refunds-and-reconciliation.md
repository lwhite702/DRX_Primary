## Find a completed sale

1. Open **Sales**.
2. Confirm the workspace and date range.
3. Search or filter by the available sale details.
4. Open the sale and verify the timestamp, operator, items, discounts, tax, total, and payment.

Use the sale record, not memory or a dashboard summary, when answering a customer question.

## Receipts and customer questions

From the sale detail, use the available receipt action to reprint or share a copy. Confirm you have the correct sale before sending anything. Avoid exposing another customer's information.

## Refunds and corrections

Refund tracking is beta. Follow the approved workspace process and your role limits. Do not delete or rewrite the original sale to make a correction look clean.

Before a refund or correction:

- Verify the sale and items.
- Confirm the amount and payment method.
- Check whether inventory should return to stock.
- Record the reason.
- Ask for manager approval when required.
- Review the resulting sale and inventory records.

If the available DRX action does not match the business policy, pause and ask an Admin rather than improvising.

## Daily reconciliation

1. Select the workspace and business date.
2. Review completed, voided, and refunded activity.
3. Compare the sales total with the approved payment or drawer record.
4. Investigate differences by opening the source sales.
5. Check refunds, discounts, credit sales, and late transactions.
6. Record the reason for any accepted difference.

## Common mismatches

- Wrong workspace or date range
- An open sale counted outside the expected period
- A void or refund not included in the comparison
- A credit sale treated like cash or card
- A duplicate retry after a delayed confirmation
- A discount or tax assumption that differs from the sale record

## If a sale appears twice

Do not create another correction until an Admin checks both sale records and payment outcomes. Capture both sale references, times, amounts, and the operator. Never include full card data in support notes.

## If a sale is missing

Refresh once, widen the date range, confirm the workspace, and check whether checkout reached the completion screen. If payment may have been taken but no sale appears, escalate before retrying.
