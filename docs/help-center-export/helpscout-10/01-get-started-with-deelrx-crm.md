## Start here

Deelrx CRM helps you keep the count right and the line moving. This guide takes you from first sign-in to a completed sale.

## Before you begin

- Use the email address connected to your DRX account.
- Ask an Admin which workspace and role you should use.
- Gather your store details and a short list of products, prices, and stock counts.
- Decide whether customer profiles will use aliases, optional contact details, or no profile at all.

## 1. Sign in and confirm your workspace

Sign in, then check the active workspace before entering data. Workspaces keep each business or location separate. If the expected workspace is missing, ask an Admin to add your account with the correct role.

## 2. Review your role

- **Admin:** manages the workspace, team access, settings, and sensitive actions.
- **Manager:** handles day-to-day operations allowed by the workspace.
- **Operator:** runs approved counter workflows with limited settings access.

If a button or page is missing, confirm your role before assuming the feature is unavailable.

## 3. Set the store basics

Open Settings and review the business profile, receipt details, tax behavior, privacy mode, and team access. Multi-store views are beta, so confirm the active workspace before sensitive actions.

## 4. Add products and stock

1. Open **Inventory** and create a product.
2. Add a clear product name, selling price, and optional SKU or barcode.
3. Open **Inventory intake** to receive stock.
4. Enter the quantity, unit cost, vendor, and batch details you track.
5. Check the product's on-hand count after saving.

Use **Inventory adjustment** only to correct a count. Always add a clear reason.

## 5. Run a first sale

1. Open **Quick Sale**.
2. Scan a barcode or search by product name or SKU.
3. Check the item, quantity, discount, tax, and total.
4. Attach a customer only when needed. An alias is enough, and checkout can continue without a customer.
5. Choose one supported payment method.
6. Choose Print, SMS, or no receipt.
7. Complete the sale and wait for confirmation before starting another.

## 6. Check the result

- Open **Sales** and confirm the transaction appears once.
- Open **Inventory** and confirm the stock count changed.
- Review the Dashboard for sales and low-stock signals.
- Check the audit trail if a count or action looks wrong.

## First-week routine

- Start each shift by checking the workspace, Dashboard, and low-stock alerts.
- End each shift by reviewing Sales and reconciliation totals.
- Keep aliases consistent and collect only customer details the customer chooses to share.
- Ask an Admin before correcting a completed sale or sensitive inventory action.

## If something goes wrong

Refresh once, confirm the workspace and role, and retry the action only if DRX did not show a completion message. Record the time, workspace, item or sale reference, and exact error before contacting support.
