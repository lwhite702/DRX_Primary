## Start with these checks

1. Read the full message on screen.
2. Confirm the active workspace.
3. Confirm your role allows the action.
4. Check the date, location, and status filters.
5. Refresh once.
6. Open the source record to see whether the action already completed.
7. Retry only when there is no completed record.

These checks prevent duplicate sales, inventory movements, and credit changes.

## Sign-in or access problems

- Confirm you are using the account invited to the workspace.
- Ask an Admin to check membership and role.
- Sign out and back in after an access change.
- If the workspace is missing, do not work in another workspace as a substitute.

## Checkout problems

Check the product, stock, customer credit, payment choice, and sale history. If payment may have been taken but the sale is missing, stop and escalate before retrying.

## Inventory problems

Check the product, workspace, on-hand count, recent movements, and audit trail. Make one approved correction only after the mismatch is understood.

## Dashboard or report problems

Match workspace, date range, location, and sale status. Verify the number in Sales or Inventory before reporting a dashboard problem.

## Customer or credit problems

Confirm the alias, workspace, attached customer, credit policy, balance, and role. Do not include customer personal information in a ticket.

## DRX Intelligence problems

Confirm the source product data, refresh once, and check permissions. Suggestions should not block checkout. Keep the current value when a suggestion looks wrong.

## What to include in a support request

- Product: Deelrx CRM
- Workspace name
- Your role
- Page or workflow
- Approximate time and time zone
- Item, sale, intake, or customer reference
- Exact error message
- What you expected and what happened
- Whether a completed record exists
- A redacted screenshot when useful

Never send passwords, card details, API keys, private keys, or unrestricted customer data.

## Support channels

Use the Help option in DRX or email `help@deelrxcrm.com`. Replies and updates should stay in the same support conversation so the history remains together.

## Urgent operating issue

If the issue could create a duplicate charge, wrong stock movement, unauthorized access, or customer-data exposure, stop the affected workflow and notify an Admin before continuing.
