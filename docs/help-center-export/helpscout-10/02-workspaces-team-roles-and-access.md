## Why the workspace matters

Each DRX workspace keeps its products, sales, customers, reports, and settings separate. Check the active workspace before checkout, inventory changes, refunds, exports, or team changes.

Multi-store views and central administration are beta. Treat the workspace indicator as the source of truth for where an action will be recorded.

## Switch workspaces safely

1. Finish or cancel any open sale or inventory form.
2. Open the workspace selector.
3. Choose the exact business or location you need.
4. Reopen the page and confirm the workspace indicator.
5. Match the date and location filters before comparing totals.

## Roles

- **Admin:** controls team access, workspace settings, privacy controls, and audit views.
- **Manager:** handles approved operational and review tasks.
- **Operator:** runs the counter and other assigned workflows.

Your workspace can limit what each role can do. A role in one workspace does not automatically grant the same access in another.

## Add or update team access

Admins can open **Settings → Team** to review members and roles. Give each person only the access needed for their job. After a role change, the member may need to sign out and sign in again.

## Fix missing data

If products, sales, customers, or reports look missing:

1. Check the active workspace.
2. Remove unexpected date or location filters.
3. Refresh the page.
4. Open the source record directly, such as Sales or Inventory.
5. Ask an Admin to confirm membership if the workspace is not listed.

## Fix an access-denied message

1. Confirm the workspace.
2. Note the exact action and message.
3. Try a read-only page to confirm basic workspace access.
4. Ask an Admin to review your role.
5. Sign out and back in after access changes.

Do not borrow another person's account or ask for broader access than the task requires.

## If an action happened in the wrong workspace

Stop before making more changes. Record the action, time, products, quantities, and amounts. Ask an Admin to review the audit trail and use the approved correction, refund, or offsetting-adjustment process. Never hide the original action.

## Access checklist

- Correct account
- Correct workspace
- Correct role
- Correct date and location filters
- No unfinished action in another workspace
- Sensitive changes reviewed in the audit trail
