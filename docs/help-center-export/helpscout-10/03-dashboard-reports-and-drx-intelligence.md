## Use the Dashboard first

The Dashboard gives a quick view of sales pace, transaction activity, and stock pressure. Use it to decide what needs attention, then open the source page before taking action.

## Daily check

1. Confirm the workspace and date range.
2. Review sales totals and transaction count.
3. Check low-stock and operational alerts.
4. Open Sales or Inventory to verify anything unusual.
5. Recheck the Dashboard after corrections or new activity.

## Reports and Insights

Reports support daily, weekly, and monthly views. Insights can show best sellers, category performance, revenue context, and low-stock pressure. Download and deeper report options remain beta, so confirm exported values against the on-screen source before sharing them.

When comparing two views, use the same workspace, location, date range, and sale status.

## DRX Intelligence

DRX Intelligence can surface pricing, demand, stock, and operational suggestions. Suggestions support decisions; they do not replace the product record, workspace policy, or operator judgment.

Before applying a suggestion:

- Confirm the product, workspace, current price, stock, and recent activity.
- Check whether cost, barcode, SKU, or vendor details are missing.
- Review the suggested change and its reason.
- Apply or override only if your role allows it.
- Add a short note when DRX asks for one.
- Check the audit trail after the change.

AI suggestions should not block checkout. If a suggestion looks wrong, leave the current value in place and review the source data.

## Missing or delayed numbers

1. Refresh once.
2. Confirm the workspace, location, and date range.
3. Verify recent sales are completed rather than open, voided, or refunded.
4. Check Sales and Inventory directly.
5. Confirm your role can view the report.

If the main totals are correct but a beta slice differs, use the main report and note the beta limitation.

## Empty or unexpected best sellers

- Expand the date range.
- Remove extra filters.
- Confirm products were sold rather than only adjusted or returned.
- Check that sales were recorded in the same workspace.

## AI suggestion problems

- Refresh after updating product data.
- Confirm edit permission.
- Complete missing product fields.
- Try one known item to see whether the issue is item-specific.
- Make an approved manual change if the suggestion cannot be applied.

If several items fail, send support one example, the workspace, the time, and the exact message. Do not include customer personal data.
