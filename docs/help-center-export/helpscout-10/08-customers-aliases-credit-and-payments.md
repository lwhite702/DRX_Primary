## Customer profiles are optional

DRX supports alias-first customer tracking. You can use a nickname or another agreed alias, keep contact fields blank, or complete a sale without attaching a customer.

Collect only what the customer chooses to share and what the business actually needs.

## Create or find a customer

1. Search for the exact alias before creating a profile.
2. Check spelling, spacing, and common variations.
3. Create a new profile only when no matching profile exists.
4. Add optional contact details only with the customer's knowledge.
5. Use the same profile on future sales to keep history together.

Notes, tags, and loyalty support are beta. Do not rely on them as the only record for a critical business decision.

## Attach a customer at checkout

Search and select the profile before payment. Confirm the alias shown in the cart. If the customer declines tracking, remove the profile or continue without one.

Sales completed without a customer will not appear in a customer profile's purchase history.

## Avoid duplicate aliases

If two profiles appear to represent the same person, ask which alias the customer wants to use and continue with that profile. Do not merge or delete customer records without the approved privacy and audit process.

## Use store credit

Credit is available only when the workspace enables it and the correct customer is attached.

1. Open the customer or Credit view.
2. Confirm available credit and current balance.
3. Attach the same customer to the sale.
4. Select Credit only when it is allowed for the full Quick Sale amount.
5. Review the remaining credit.
6. Complete the sale once.
7. Confirm both the sale and customer credit activity.

Quick Sale does not promise split tender or standalone payments toward an existing credit balance.

## Credit is unavailable

- Confirm the correct customer is attached.
- Confirm the workspace's credit policy.
- Check available credit.
- Confirm your role can use or adjust credit.
- Review recent credit activity for a prior change.

## Purchase history looks incomplete

Confirm the workspace and alias. Check whether the original sales were attached to that profile. Unattached sales remain valid but will not appear under the customer.

## Protect customer information

Do not place phone numbers, email addresses, notes, payment details, or other personal information in support tickets unless support explicitly requests a safe field. Use the customer or sale reference instead.
