# Getting Started: Your First Week in DRX

DRX is built to help you sell fast, track inventory clean, and keep customer data under control without the noise.

## What this does

This guide gets your shop from fresh setup to daily use with the least friction.

## Who can use it

Owner, Manager

## Before you start

- Make sure your business workspace is created.
- Confirm owner account access works.
- Decide who needs manager vs staff access.

## Step-by-step

1. Open `Dashboard` and confirm you can load business data.
2. Go to `Settings` and verify basic business details.
3. Open `Settings > Team` and invite staff accounts.
4. Add products from `Inventory`.
5. Set alias shortcuts in `Settings > Inventory > Aliases`.
6. Run a test checkout from `Quick Sale`.
7. Check `Sales` history to confirm the sale posted.

## What happens next

You move into normal daily flow: Quick Sale, inventory updates, customer lookup, and end-of-day report checks.

## Tips

- Start simple: add your most sold products first.
- Keep aliases short so staff can move fast on mobile.

## Common mistakes

- Giving everyone manager-level access by default.
- Skipping test sales before going live.

## Troubleshooting

- If `Quick Sale` won’t start, check auth/session and role access.
- If products don’t show in sale flow, check product status in `Inventory`.
