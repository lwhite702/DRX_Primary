# Inventory Audit: Fix Count Problems Fast

When the numbers feel off, this is the cleanup playbook.

## What this does

Shows how to audit inventory movement and isolate bad counts.

## Who can use it

Manager, Owner

## Before you start

- Have access to inventory history and recent sales.

## Step-by-step

1. Open `Inventory > Audit`.
2. Identify products with unexpected deltas.
3. Compare movement against `Sales` history.
4. Check recent `Intake` and `Adjust` actions.
5. Apply corrective adjustment with a clear reason.

## What happens next

Corrected counts stabilize POS and restore trust in reports.

## Tips

- Fix high-volume items first.
- Keep one person accountable for final correction approval per shift.

## Common mistakes

- Editing values without documenting cause.

## Troubleshooting

- If mismatch repeats, verify alias/sku collisions and duplicate product usage.
