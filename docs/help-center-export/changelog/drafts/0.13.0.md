# DRX 0.13.0 customer changelog draft

Status: Draft - publication requires approval and release evidence.

Source implementation notes:

### Added

- **Customer store credit accounts and inventory insights upgrade (WRE-208)**: Added dedicated modals for extending store credit, recording credit payments, and managing persistent customer alert banners, alongside timestamped customer notes and AI limit recommendations. Added a FIFO stock batch inspection drawer, expiring-batch endpoints, and inventory forecast insights across the web operator experience.
- **POS customer lookup, split tenders, sales history, and delivery dispatch (WRE-199)**: Added real-time POS customer lookup with normalized phone/alias search, recent customer favorite items, and quick-add modal; expanded tender support for split payments and P2P methods (Cash App, PayPal, Zelle, Venmo) with card processor fee policies; added configurable delivery fee split models, dispatch modes, ETA updates, and Uber tracking URLs; and added full sales history inspection with tender and override breakdowns.
- **Customer continuity through checkout (WRE-166)**: Allow authorized operators to quick-create a privacy-scoped customer during payment without losing the sale, and link an anonymous completed sale to a customer from native transaction review. Customer attribution updates purchase and loyalty totals and linked delivery records atomically with an audit event; sale settlement, inventory, and credit entries remain unchanged. Credit-sale reassignment remains blocked.
- **Release-integrity unique version enforcement (WRE-233)**: Hardened `scripts/release/integrity-check.mjs` to reject `Release impact: none` when runtime product files change, require `VERSION` and native `CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION` build increments on product PRs and `main` pushes, allow changelog validation via either `[Unreleased]` or a newly cut release heading matching `VERSION`, and enforce `mintlify/CHANGELOG.mdx` parity.

### Fixed

- **Exact dollar-amount fractional checkout (WRE-215)**: Preserve requested merchandise cents through the server quote and sale record while recording the operator-confirmed quantity. Reconcile the checkout subtotal to the requested amount after the signed price adjustment, flag differences from normal pricing without requiring manager approval, and use confirmed quantity for stock deduction and FIFO COGS. Authenticated end-to-end and real-device validation remain release gates; historical sales were not modified because their original requested amount is not stored.
- **POS pricing overrides, session revocation, commerce preset inputs, and reports summary (WRE-208)**: Fixed fractional price override calculation in full checkout and Convex commerce layer; added server fallback and Clerk secret key forwarding for security session revocation; preserved raw string state while typing weight and price presets and locked unit selection when stock exists; and consolidated sales ledger summaries and operating cutoff windows in reports.
- **POS delivery validation, split tender allocation, and preview auth guards (WRE-199)**: Validated delivery tracking URLs and vendor fees before normalization, synchronized vendor fee updates to linked sales, enforced exact split-tender cent allocation, cleared stale payment references on non-card/P2P tenders, and hardened production/preview authentication environment checks.
- **Production health probe and Convex URL normalization (WRE-202, WRE-203, WRE-204, WRE-205)**: Stripped unsupported CLI flags from production smoke checks, allowed public health probe fallback when `HEALTHCHECK_SECRET` is unset, and sanitized surrounding quotes and trailing slashes from configured Convex URLs.
- **Native iOS error states and navigation toolbars (WRE-193)**: Standardized `ContentUnavailableView` error states and retry actions across native iOS tabs and modernized navigation toolbars to iOS 17+ `topBarTrailing` placements.
- **Home setup progress (WRE-168)**: Scope the first-product checklist lookup to the active business and show a retry prompt when setup progress cannot be refreshed.
