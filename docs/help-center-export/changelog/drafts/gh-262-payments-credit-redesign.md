# Payments and customer credit screens — customer changelog draft

Status: Draft - publish only after release approval and verified release evidence.

### Improved

- Find a customer in Payments by the name or alias shown in the list, even when their account is on another page. See how much they owe, how long the oldest unpaid amount has been due, and a short breakdown by age. Clear notices identify totals or due dates that still need review. Customer profiles now use the same balance and payment history, and payment actions and suggested credit settings use clearer labels.
