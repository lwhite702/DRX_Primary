# Deelrx CRM Microcopy

Use this file for quick patterns. The full rules are in `BRAND_VOICE.md`, and
the source values used by the apps are in `packages/content/src/index.ts`.

## Confirmations

- Sale saved.
- Payment added.
- Marked paid.
- Customer added.
- Stock updated.
- Note saved.
- Invite sent.
- Changes saved.

Light humor is fine only when the result is safe and clear:

- Paid in full. We love to see it.
- Saved. Your memory gets the day off.
- Stock updated. No mystery math.

## Empty states

- No customers yet. Add the people you sell to most.
- No products yet. Add your first product and start keeping count.
- No sales yet. Start your first sale.
- Nothing due. Everybody is paid up. Nice.
- No history yet. Changes will show here.

## Errors and offline states

- That didn’t work. Try again.
- That sale didn’t save. Your details are still here. Try again.
- That payment didn’t go through. Check the amount and try again.
- You’re offline. Check your connection and try again.
- We couldn’t load this. Try again.

State what failed and what to do next. Say whether entered information is still
there when that is true. Do not use humor for money, access, privacy, security,
deletion, or data loss.

## Privacy

- Privacy
- Hide details
- Protect details
- What’s saved
- Delete old records
- Choose what your team can see.
- DRX collects less and protects what you add.

Do not promise perfect privacy, anonymity, or security.
