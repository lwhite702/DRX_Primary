# DRX customer copy drafts

These templates are drafts. They do not authorize provider, campaign, store, or
public Help Center changes.

## Help Center terminology and short answers

Use the approved labels **Home**, **Products**, **Stock**, **New sale**,
**Sales**, **Payments**, **Balance due**, **Team member**, **Business**,
**Location**, and **Trends**. Keep route names and internal identifiers out of
customer instructions. Link to repository Help Center sources with relative
links; do not point readers to retired Featurebase pages.

- **Where do I check what sold?** Open **Sales** and check the saved sale.
- **How do I record goods that arrived?** Open **Products**, choose **Add
  stock**, enter the counted quantity, and check the saved stock history.
- **A payment result is unclear. What now?** Check **Sales** or **Payments** for
  the saved record before trying again. Do not create a second record to test.

## Email

### Weekly numbers

- Subject: Your weekly numbers are ready
- Preview: See what sold, what you made, and what is running low.
- Heading: Here’s your week
- Button: Check your numbers

### Balance reminder

- Subject: A balance is still due
- Preview: Open Payments to see the amount and record what was paid.
- Heading: This balance is still open
- Button: View balance

### New sign-in

- Subject: New sign-in to DRX
- Preview: Check the sign-in time. If it wasn’t you, change your password and
  contact support.
- Heading: Was this you?
- Button: Review account

### Delivery reminder

- Subject: A delivery needs a look
- Preview: Open Deliveries to check its status.
- Heading: Check this delivery
- Button: Open Deliveries

### Product update

- Subject: A DRX update is ready
- Preview: See what changed and whether you need to do anything.
- Heading: What changed
- Button: See what changed

Keep account, balance, customer, and product details out of email subject lines
and previews. Add those details only after sign-in when the recipient is
authorized to see them.

## Push

- Low stock: **Stock is running low** / Open DRX to review Products.
- Balance due: **A balance needs a look** / Open Payments to review it.
- Weekly report: **Your weekly numbers are ready** / Open DRX to see the report.
- Delivery: **A delivery needs a look** / Open Deliveries to check its status.
- Security: **New sign-in to DRX** / Open DRX to review account activity.
- Product update: **DRX has an update** / Open DRX to see what changed.

Never put customer names, contact details, notes, balances, or product details on
the lock screen. The app may show private details only after account access.
Keep push text useful when the operating system hides its preview.

## Sales one-liner

Deelrx CRM helps independent sellers keep customers, products, stock, sales,
payments, and balances due in one place.

## Support replies

### First reply

We can help. Tell us which screen you were on, what you tried, and what you
expected to happen. Include the approximate time and your device type. Don’t send
passwords, verification codes, payment credentials, customer details, or private
business records.

### Unclear save or payment

Please check **Sales** or **Payments** for a saved record before trying again.
If you still can’t confirm the result, reply with the approximate time, the
screen you used, and the message you saw. Leave the original records unchanged
while we review it.

### Access issue

Check that you’re signed in to the right account and **Business**. Ask the
business owner to confirm your access. Don’t share sign-in codes or another
person’s account details with support.
