# DRX provider copy templates

These templates are drafts. They do not authorize provider, campaign, store, or
public Help Center changes.

## Email

### Weekly numbers

- Subject: Your weekly numbers are ready
- Preview: See what sold, what you made, and what is running low.
- Heading: Here’s your week
- Button: Check your numbers

### Balance reminder

- Subject: A balance is still due
- Preview: Open Payments to see the amount and record what was paid.
- Heading: This balance is still open
- Button: View balance

### New sign-in

- Subject: New sign-in to DRX
- Preview: Check the device and time. If it was not you, secure your account.
- Heading: Was this you?
- Button: Check account

## Push

- Low stock: You’re running low / Two products are below their stock limit.
- Balance due: Three balances are still due / Open Payments to check them.
- Weekly report: Your weekly numbers are ready / See what sold and what you made.
- Delivery: Delivery update / Open Deliveries for the latest status.

Never put customer names, contact details, notes, balances, or product details on
the lock screen. The app may show private details only after account access.

## Sales one-liner

Deelrx CRM helps independent sellers keep customers, products, stock, sales,
payments, and balances due in one place.

## Support reply opening

We can help. Tell us what you were trying to do and what happened. Do not send
customer details, payment information, or private records.

## Product update

- Heading: [Clear result]
- Body: [What changed]. [Who has it]. [Anything the user needs to do].
- Button: See what changed

## Store listing lead

Keep track of customers, stock, sales, payments, and money owed. DRX helps you
see what came in, what went out, and what you made.
